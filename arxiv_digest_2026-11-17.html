Decoding SO(5) Deconfined Multicriticality: Conformal Bootstrap and Fuzzy-Sphere Spectroscopy
Zhijin Li, Tinghong Shen 2611.20214v1 [PDF] Score 12
CFThep-thcond-mat.stat-mechcond-mat.str-el
The nature of the deconfined quantum critical point (DQCP) remains unsettled: competing explanations invoke a real multicritical conformal field theory (CFT) or pseudocriticality governed by nearby complex fixed points. We use the conformal bootstrap and fuzzy-sphere regularization to compute $SO(5)$ CFT data and compare our results with large-scale quantum Monte Carlo (QMC) data to investigate these scenarios. Using mixed correlators of the lowest vector $φ$, singlet $s$, and traceless symmetric scalar $t$, we characterize the bootstrap cone whose tip lies remarkably close to the position indicated by large-scale QMC. We extract an extensive spectrum across $SO(5)$ irreducible representations, together with operator product expansion (OPE) coefficients. We study a fuzzy-sphere realization of the $SO(5)$ nonlinear sigma model with a level-1 Wess--Zumino--Witten term. Using the special conformal generators, we identify 52 conformal primaries in a system with \(N=10\) Landau orbitals, including 31 not previously classified as primaries in the earlier fuzzy-sphere study. Symmetry projection extends fuzzy-sphere exact diagonalization to $N=12$. The bootstrap spectrum and OPE coefficients show close agreement with fuzzy-sphere data, including 16 of the newly identified primaries in channels accessible to the mixed-correlator bootstrap. The lowest $SO(5)$ singlet scalar is relevant in the bootstrap and QMC results; its fuzzy-sphere data is $Δ_s=2.816$ at $N=12$ and decreases with system size. Our results suggest that the mixed-correlator bootstrap solution represents a unitary CFT with a relevant $SO(5)$ singlet scalar and is closely connected to the QMC and fuzzy-sphere data for the DQCP. The conformal bootstrap, fuzzy-sphere, and QMC together provide comprehensive evidence for an $SO(5)$ multicritical CFT and critical data to decode the phases in two-dimensional quantum magnets.

Higher-point amplitudes and recursion relations from the worldsheet in complex Liouville string
Aleksandr Artemev 2611.21091v1 [PDF] Score 6
String Theoryhep-th
We consider multipoint perturbative string amplitudes in genus zero in complex Liouville string theory (CLS) in the worldsheet approach. Based on explicit worldsheet calculations and analytic bootstrap, we first compute correlators involving ground ring operators, which additionally depend on a choice of integration cycle on the moduli space. Using this result and higher equations of motion in Liouville theory, we calculate higher-point tachyon correlators with one degenerate field and explain what contributions were missed in previous attempts to apply this method in the literature. This allows us to derive a recursion formula, which agrees with the general recursion for amplitudes known from "matrix model" approach to CLS. We also propose an expression for correlators with ground ring insertions in terms of tautological classes on the moduli space of curves and comment on generalization of these results to the case of rational minimal strings.

Equivariant Localization with Electric Monodromy in M-theory
Ji-seong Chae 2611.20117v1 [PDF] Score 6
AdS/CFTCFTString Theoryhep-th
We extend equivariant localization for supersymmetric AdS$_3$ backgrounds of M-theory to solutions with nonzero electric flux periods. The associated local electric potential acquires additive monodromy, contributing to the warped volume alongside R-symmetry fixed points and outer boundaries. Using Killing-spinor identities, we relate the electric periods to magnetic flux and, for a class of compact backgrounds, the warped volume to the electric Maxwell charge. We also derive a relation between the gravitational anomaly and the electric flux. We apply these results to AdS$_3$ $\times S^3 \times S^3 \times T^2$ and AdS$_3$ $\times S^3 \times K3 \times S^1$, recovering their central charges and deriving the R-current levels of the former directly from eleven-dimensional supergravity. Finally, we study a noncompact half-BPS AdS$_3$ $\times S^3\times S^3$ geometry fibered over a periodic half-cylinder, where fixed-point, monodromy and outer-boundary contributions coexist.

Two-scale system: compactified ambient metrics and Scri/Spi/Ti
Xavier Bekaert, Maxim Grigoriev, Mikhail Markov 2611.19377v1 [PDF] Score 6
Celestial HolographyString Theorygr-qchep-thmath-ph
We show that certain geometric constructions, including the Fefferman-Graham ambient metric, Brinkmann spaces, and ultrastatic vacuum spacetimes, admit natural conformal compactifications and can be unified within a single framework arising naturally from conformal geometry. The framework is based on a two-scale system comprising two weight-one conformal scalars satisfying almost-Einstein equations and certain regularity conditions. As a particular instance of this unifying framework, we obtain a new ambient construction for Spi and Ti, the extended spatial and temporal infinities of Ricci-flat spacetimes. A remarkable feature of this construction is that Spi/Ti and the null infinity of the corresponding spacetimes are naturally realised as a boundary face and the corner of the two-scale ambient space, respectively. We also demonstrate how the two-scale system can serve as a background ambient geometry for studying the asymptotic behaviour of (gauge) fields at spatial, temporal, and null infinity.

Learning from Duality in Neural Network Field Theories
Pietro Capuozzo, Brandon Robinson, Benjamin Suzzoni 2611.21503v1 [PDF] Score 5

We develop neural realizations of positive-level Wess-Zumino-Witten models using bosonic Wakimoto and fermionic Fourier-Berezin fields. Level-rank duality connects the charged sectors of the fermionic construction, where explicit neural observables recover conformal block data and enable the transfer of information about unknown correlators between paired theories. We also show that distinct neural presentations of the same continuum current algebra exhibit different finite-width behavior, with approximation accuracy depending on architecture, regulator, and parameter distribution. These results connect the exact mathematical structure of conformal field theory with the expressive and computational properties of neural field theories.

Lifting asymptotic symmetries from metric to tetrad formulation
Antoine Vincenti 2611.21296v1 [PDF] Score 5
Asymptotic Symmetrieshep-th
Metric solution spaces defined by gauge and boundary conditions are associated with a residual symmetry algebra, consisting of the diffeomorphism generators whose action preserves these conditions. Describing a metric solution space in terms of tetrad variables adds a local Lorentz freedom. When part of this freedom is fixed by additional tetrad gauge and boundary conditions, metric residual symmetries must be supplemented by compensating Lorentz transformations. If a residual Lorentz freedom remains, there is an ambiguity in these compensating transformations. The most direct choices generally fail to realize the metric residual symmetry algebra as a subalgebra of the tetrad one, because of a field-dependent cocycle. We show that a reference tetrad, that is a section of the tetrad solution space seen as a principal bundle over the metric one, always provides a lift free of this cocycle, so that the tetrad residual symmetry algebra is the semi-direct sum of the metric one and the ideal of field-dependent residual Lorentz transformations. The construction is independent of the gauge conditions, of the residual group, of the first-order Lagrangian, and reduces to the Kosmann lift on metric isometry generators. We illustrate it on a Weyl BMS-type solution space with time-dependent boundary metric, described in tetrad variables with a residual U(1) Lorentz freedom, for which the cocycle of a naive lift is computed and then explicitly removed.

Quantum chaos from time-dependent CFT states at large central charge
Shaun D. Hampton, Pantelis Panopoulos, Gideon Vos 2611.20817v1 [PDF] Score 5
CFThep-th
We provide a first study of the behavior of four probes on heavy, time-dependent background states of conformal field theories at large central charge. Employing the monodromy method and including subleading corrections to the identity block, we compute the correlation function of two light and two middle-heavy operators on such a heavy background, by switching on a small interaction between the light and middle-heavy sectors. Summing the discontinuity contributions and analytically continuing from Euclidean to Lorentzian signature, we show that the out-of-time-ordered correlator (OTOC) exhibits maximal chaos, that is, the Lyapunov exponent saturates the MSS bound.

Holographic Homogeneous Black Branes from Anisotropic Scaling
Markus A. G. Amano 2611.20673v1 [PDF] Score 4
AdS/CFThep-thgr-qc
We construct homogeneous anisotropic black branes in Einstein gravity and Einstein--Maxwell--dilaton theory in arbitrary spacetime dimensions. By imposing compatibility between radial scaling and the spatial Lie algebra, we reduce the field equations to radial equations and algebraic constraints on the scaling exponents, curvature, and matter fields. We construct neutral, electric, magnetic, and dyonic solutions, including explicit five-dimensional Bianchi geometries and higher-dimensional families supported by Ricci nilsolitons. We investigate the thermodynamics of these solutions, obtaining their Euclidean action, mass, and entropy scaling. We also study the dynamics of a massive probe scalar, deriving an asymptotic mass bound and an exact purely damped quasinormal spectrum for a particular class of scaling geometries. Our results establish a framework for studying thermodynamic and dynamical properties of homogeneous anisotropic scaling geometries that are generally not asymptotically locally anti-de Sitter.

Negative and Positive Saddle-Selection Ambiguities in the HTEE
H. Ebrahim, M. Ali-Akbari, K. Taheranvarian 2611.19562v1 [PDF] Score 4
AdS/CFTEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
The holographic timelike entanglement entropy (HTEE), computed via complexified extremal surfaces, suffers from a saddle-selection ambiguity: for sufficiently small time intervals (\(Δt\to0\)), both a vacuum-connected and a vacuum-disconnected complex extremal surface contribute, and the minimal-real-part criterion and the vacuum-reduction criterion can favor different saddles. We study this ambiguity in strongly coupled field theories whose dual geometries are hyperscaling-violating Lifshitz backgrounds, in which conformal symmetry and Lorentz invariance are broken by the Lifshitz exponent and the hyperscaling-violation parameter. Working in \(2+1\)- and \(3+1\)-dimensional dual field theories, we derive general expressions for the HTEE in the vacuum-connected and vacuum-disconnected saddles, \(s_{v.c.}\) and \(s_{v.d.}\), respectively, and compare their real parts. We find that the two criteria agree everywhere except at the first accidental zero. The ambiguity appears in two distinct forms. In the \emph{negative} family, \(\operatorname{Re}(s_{v.c.})=0\) and \(\operatorname{Re}(s_{v.d.})<0\), whereas in the \emph{positive} family, \(\operatorname{Re}(s_{v.c.})>0\) and \(\operatorname{Re}(s_{v.d.})=0\). The latter shows that the ambiguity survives even in the conformal \(3+1\)-dimensional case. It therefore seems that the saddle-selection ambiguity is a generic feature of the complexified-geometry prescription.

Instant Folded Strings: Fractal Decay and ANEC Saturation
Nissan Itzhaki, Uri Peleg 2611.21424v1 [PDF] Score 3
String Theoryhep-th
Instant Folded Strings (IFSs) present a puzzle. On the one hand, they appear to be an intrinsic part of string theory: they are fundamental closed strings nucleated classically in backgrounds in which the string coupling grows toward the future, and they have been argued to play an important role in black-hole interiors and cosmology. On the other hand, their energy-momentum tensor violates the Averaged Null Energy Condition (ANEC), placing IFSs in tension with standard causality and unitarity arguments. We show that finite string coupling resolves this tension by triggering a decay cascade that saturates the ANEC in Minkowski spacetime. The remnant of the original IFS persists indefinitely, continuously seeding a self-similar fragmentation cascade that produces infinitely many strings over its full history. In the semiclassical regime, the cascade is governed by a single Malthusian exponent, $κ_{\mathrm{IFS}}=2\sqrt{2}-2$, which controls the branching growth, pressure decay, cutoff-ANEC convergence, and anomalous non-integer dependence on the string coupling. The complete state has vanishing total energy and momentum while remaining highly excited and carrying negative integrated pressure on every finite time slice. This provides a concrete example of genuinely stringy low-energy dynamics in which microscopic null-energy consistency coexists with macroscopic negative pressure.

Thermal deep inelastic scattering, universality and complex geodesics
Andrei Parnachev 2611.21127v1 [PDF] Score 3
AdS/CFTCFThep-thhep-lathep-ph
In deep inelastic scattering (DIS), a lepton scatters off a target hadron whose structure is probed by an exchanged photon with large spacelike virtuality. We consider two-point functions of currents in strongly interacting quantum field theories at finite temperature in this kinematic regime and use a holographic description to compute them. At large virtuality, the crossover from vacuum-dominated to thermally dominated physics corresponds to a transition from exponentially suppressed absorption to power-law behavior of the response functions. This regime is related to a particular near-lightcone limit of thermal CFT correlators. We show that the response functions exhibit a large degree of universality: the dependence on the three-point couplings of currents to the stress tensor enters through a channel-dependent coefficient multiplying the kinematic parameter that controls the relative size of vacuum and thermal effects. The same coefficient controls conformal collider bounds; when either bound is saturated, the corresponding response function takes its vacuum form in the near-lightcone limit. Furthermore, the absorption exponent in the low-temperature regime is related to a complex geodesic propagating in the near-lightcone geometry.

Casimir-like wormholes
Mauricio Cataldo 2611.20168v1 [PDF] Score 3
Entanglement & Geometrygr-qchep-th
We investigate spherically symmetric spacetimes sourced by matter with energy density $ρ\propto 1/r^{4}$ and equation of state $p_r = ωρ$. We derive the most general solution of Einstein's equations for this class of sources, obtaining black holes, naked singularities and traversable wormholes, parametrized by the integration constants and $ω$. We show that only two classes of matter can support traversable wormholes: Casimir-type matter with $ω> 0$ and phantom matter with $ω< -1$, while quintessence and conventional dark energy are excluded by the flare-out condition. For the Casimir value $ω= 3$, the transverse pressure approaches $p_t \to -ρ$ near the central singularity, so the full algebraic structure of the Casimir stress-energy tensor, $ρ\propto -1/r^{4}$, $p_r = 3ρ$, $p_t = -ρ$, is not imposed but emerges from Einstein's equations. Treating the amplitude of the energy density as a free classical parameter, we obtain wormholes with throat radii from microscopic to astrophysical scales. These Casimir-like configurations share the functional form of the Casimir tensor but are not generated by the physical Casimir effect; Garattini's Planck-scale Casimir wormhole is recovered when the quantum Casimir energy scale is imposed. The total amount of exotic matter is finite for arbitrary $ω$ and scales linearly with the throat radius. Analyzing the traveler's acceleration, tidal forces and transit times, we show that radial free fall satisfies all human traversability conditions for $r_0 \gtrsim 10^{8}$ m. Finally, embedding diagrams illustrate three geometric regimes depending on $ω$.

Semra Gurtas Dogan, Abdullah Guvendi, Hassan Hassanabadi 2611.19935v1 [PDF] Score 3
AdS/CFTgr-qchep-th
We compute the massless scalar quasinormal spectra of charged Letelier black holes surrounded by logarithmic perfect fluid dark matter (PFDM) and quintessence, for $Λ=0$ and for asymptotically anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetimes, together with the test Maxwell spectra for $Λ=0$. For $Λ=0$, sixth order WKB values with Padé resummation agree with independent time domain evolutions to within $0.06\%$ for the fundamental modes. Stronger string cloud and quintessence lower both the oscillation frequencies and the damping rates, while a larger charge or a larger positive PFDM parameter $β$ raises both. In our convention $β>0$ means negative PFDM energy density; negative $β$ gives a positive density and reverses the shift. The angular frequency and Lyapunov exponent of the unstable circular null orbit reproduce the eikonal trends and approximate the $\ell=2$ WKB frequencies at the percent level. For $Λ<0$ the scalar potential diverges at the AdS boundary and the single barrier WKB formula does not apply. We therefore solve the eigenvalue problem with a Chebyshev pseudospectral method in ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates, with regularity at the horizon and a Dirichlet condition at the boundary, and check the modes against time domain evolutions and Schwarzschild-AdS benchmarks. The AdS spectrum contains long-lived modes trapped between the photon sphere barrier and the reflecting boundary, with damping rates spanning about five orders of magnitude. The damping correlates with the ratio of the oscillation frequency to the barrier top frequency, consistent with leakage through the barrier. No growing modes are found in the test field sectors and parameter ranges studied, which does not establish the stability of the fully coupled system.

A Numerical Approach to Quantization
Grace Murray, Siyue Zhu, Angeline Hu et al. 2611.19646v1 [PDF] Score 3
String Theoryquant-phhep-th
We introduce a numerical approach that simultaneously searches for matrix quantizations and compatible Poisson structures on classical geometric surfaces. Our method identifies irreducible Hermitian matrix representations and a trainable Poisson multiplier by minimizing a loss function that penalizes violations of the Casimir and commutation constraints. Through numerical investigations and subsequent theoretical analysis, we establish a no-go theorem showing that, for Weyl quantized fuzzy spheres with nondegenerate axial spectrum, the matrix dimension cannot exceed the degree of the nonconstant polynomial axial Poisson multiplier. Although here we focus on Weyl quantized fuzzy spheres, the framework is extensible to general geometric manifolds and quantization schemes. Our approach also provides a practical starting point for investigating Schild string theory and the quantization of spacetime histories.

Entanglement & Geometrygr-qc
We study an ultrastatic, spherically symmetric family of traversable wormholes obtained by locally deforming a generalized Ellis--Bronnikov areal-radius profile in a spacetime with an asymptotic angular deficit. Depending on the deformation parameters, the same analytic family contains one, two, three, or four throats. The corresponding bifurcation curves are obtained in dimensionless form, and the resulting geometries are regular and asymptotically conical. We also reconstruct a source in General Relativity using a canonical scalar, a shift-symmetric phantom scalar, and a magnetic nonlinear electromagnetic field coupled to the canonical scalar. In the reference multi-throat geometry, the coupling removes a branch obstruction of an uncoupled single-valued nonlinear electrodynamics (NED); the same obstruction occurs whenever equal values of the magnetic invariant require different values of $\mathcal G_F$. For $\mathcal G(F)=F+λ\sqrt F$, the NED sector carries the leading asymptotic stress, which scales as $r^{-2}$ and approaches the string-cloud equation of state. The total stress tensor satisfies pointwise energy conditions in finite belly-centered regions of representative two- and four-throat solutions, while the throats violate the radial null energy condition (NEC) and the violation extends into integrable asymptotic tails. The Nandi--Zhang--Kumar covariant volume integral of $ρ+p_r$ is quadratic in the deformation amplitude; its zero is distinct from the bifurcation curves. Away from saddle-node boundaries, each throat supports a radially unstable background-metric light ring, and each intermediate belly supports a radially stable one, with an internally trapped null family in the double-throat case. These results place the topology, source reconstruction, integrated NEC content, and background-metric circular-orbit structure in a common parameter-space description.
