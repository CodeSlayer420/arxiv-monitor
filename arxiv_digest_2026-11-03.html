Smoothing the Bouncing-Geodesic Singularity in AdS/CFT
Mingxuan Liu, Shan-Ming Ruan 2611.03347v1 [PDF] Score 15
AdS/CFTCFTQuantum Gravityhep-th
Black hole singularities remain a central open problem in quantum gravity. In the AdS/CFT correspondence, bulk spacelike extremal surfaces can probe the black hole interior, and information about the near-singularity geometry is encoded in analytically continued boundary correlators. As the bouncing geodesic approaches the black hole singularity, it becomes nearly null and gives rise to characteristic divergences at complex times. Importantly, these complex-time singularities emerge only in the strict semiclassical limit ($G_N\to 0$ or $c\to\infty$) and are governed by the asymptotically linear quasinormal mode (QNM) spectrum. At finite $c$, nonlinear deformations of the QNM trajectories produce suppression kernel that smooths out the complex-time singularities. Using Liouville CFT as an analytically solvable example, we explicitly demonstrate the finite-$c$ deformation of the QNM spectrum and its associated pole-zero structure. We also identify a complementary Gaussian smoothing mechanism arising from finite-energy fluctuations in the thermal spectral decomposition. These results show how finite $G_N$ quantum corrections modify the holographic imprints of the classical black hole singularity.

The Virasoro Bootstrap: from the $c\sim1$ desert to coupled Potts
Stefanos R. Kousvos, Alessandro Piazza, Alessandro Vichi 2611.03565v1 [PDF] Score 11
CFThep-thcond-mat.stat-mechcond-mat.str-el
We develop a numerical conformal bootstrap for two-dimensional CFTs with $c>1$ based on Virasoro conformal blocks, computed with Zamolodchikov's recursion and optimal polynomial interpolation. As a first test we isolate an island around three decoupled Ising models. We then consider three 3-state Potts models coupled through their energy operators, a candidate irrational CFT. Mixed correlators isolate the decoupled theory in an island, and different gap assumptions, motivated by perturbation theory, yield a peninsula that contains the coupled fixed point but not the decoupled one. Finally, we explore the standard single-correlator bound on the lowest-dimension scalar primary in the OPE of two identical scalars. We find that for central charges just above $c=1$ the bound collapses over a wide range of external dimensions. This excludes any theory whose lightest scalar is absent from its own OPE and lies in this range, and supports the conjecture that no compact CFT exists in this region. We explain several features of these bounds analytically.

What crossing symmetry forbids just above $c=1$
Yu Nakayama 2611.03217v1 [PDF] Score 10
CFThep-th
What does a unitary two-dimensional conformal field theory look like just above $c=1$? Using the numerical conformal bootstrap, we study the four-point function of a scalar Virasoro primary $φ$ such that no primary lighter than $φ$ appears in its operator product expansion with itself. Slightly above $c=1$, crossing symmetry excludes the interval $0.17\lesssimΔ_φ\lesssim1$, although the compact boson realizes every such $Δ_φ$ at $c=1$. Combining this exclusion with results from the modular bootstrap, we show that the lightest primary of a compact unitary conformal field theory at $c=1.0005$ or $1.001$ has dimension $Δ_{\rm gap}<0.17$. Further from $c=1$, the largest excluded central charge at fixed $Δ_φ$ has two sharp minima near $Δ_φ=1/2$ and $4/7$. At these values it lies within $2\times10^{-7}$ of $c=8/7$, where the $\mathbb{Z}_5$ parafermion and its charge-conjugation orbifold have scalars of exactly these dimensions.

A Matrix View of Holographic Null-State Equations in $d\geq 2$
Kuo-Wei Huang 2611.02855v1 [PDF] Score 7
AdS/CFTCFThep-th
We show that the null-state-type partial differential equations recently derived from AdS gravity with a black hole can be efficiently organized using finite-dimensional matrices with systematic off-diagonal structures. This matrix formulation generates an infinite tower of CFT differential equations at arbitrary levels. The $d=2$ matrix reproduces a known result based on Virasoro null-state vectors at large central charge and admits an algebraic representation in terms of a linear combination of the identity matrix and $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ generators, with a heavy-state deformation encoding the central charge contribution. The $d=4$ governing matrix exhibits a similar yet richer structure involving products of $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ generators. We also discuss recursion relations and factorized product structures associated with these holographic differential equations.

AdS/CFTCFThep-phhep-th
The description of heavy quarks inside the QGP medium, especially at low momenta, remains challenging from first principles, due to large coupling strength and gluon occupation numbers. An alternative way to formulate a description in terms of perturbative degrees of freedom is the interaction of the heavy quarks with the collective excitations of the medium in the form of phonons. This description is derived as an effective field theory (EFT) and therefore allows for a natural occurring power counting of the allowed interactions. This EFT has already been established in our previous work, where we have derived the phonon-heavy quark interactions and calculated their thermal scattering cross section. Finally, we will use these coefficients to solve the Boltzmann equation for the heavy quark distribution, studying its thermalization in the QGP via soft phonon interactions.

We consider generalized de Sitter entropy which includes both entanglement entropy of the strongly coupled quantum field and the gravitational Wald entropy in the framework of holographic semiclassical gravity. Spin-dependent topological structure of black-hole thermodynamics
Erdem Sucu, İzzet Sakallı, Yusuf Sucu et al. 2611.02035v1 [PDF] Score 6
AdS/CFTGeometric GravityQuantum Gravitygr-qc
Thermodynamic topology classifies black-hole equilibrium states through topological defects of an off-shell thermodynamic field, while quantum-corrected Hawking radiation can retain information about the quantum numbers of the emitted particle. Whether these two structures are connected has remained largely unexplored. In this work we investigate this question for a massive-gravity AdS black hole by combining arbitrary-spin zitterbewegung tunneling with generalized-uncertainty-principle corrections. We derive the particle-spin-dependent Hawking temperature, construct the corresponding channel-dependent effective entropy through the first law, and formulate the associated off-shell free energy and thermodynamic vector field. As reference sectors, we also analyze the Bekenstein-Hawking and exponentially corrected entropies. We find a clear separation between local and global thermodynamic topology. For the $γ=-1$ branch, a single positively wound defect persists, yielding $W=+1$, whereas for $γ=+1$ the defects occur in oppositely wound pairs and the global charge remains $W=0$. The particle spin nevertheless shifts the defect positions and the critical configurations at which the $γ=+1$ pair merges and annihilates. The fold separating the two-defect and defect-free regions is controlled jointly by the particle spin, the GUP parameter, and the cosmological constant. Thus, the quantum properties of the emitted particle do not alter the global topological class, but they reorganize the local defect and bifurcation structure within it. Our results establish a direct connection between quantum-corrected Hawking radiation and the corresponding effective off-shell thermodynamic topology, showing that its local defect structure can retain information about the quantum channel through which the black hole radiates.

Symmetry breaking from conformality loss on holographic Rényi defects
Siqing Yu 2611.03413v1 [PDF] Score 5
AdS/CFThep-thcond-mat.str-elgr-qc
The Rényi entropies of a ball are computed by a conformal defect, the Rényi defect, dual in holography to a hyperbolic black hole. At an index $n_*$ its charged operator merges with its alternate-quantization partner, and the defect loses conformality. Beyond $n_*$ it flows with no source to a conformal defect that breaks the U(1) symmetry; below $n_*$ that defect needs a symmetry-breaking source, so at large $N$, conformality loss makes the breaking spontaneous. The scalar hair beyond $n_*$, expected to break the symmetries of hyperbolic space, thus preserves them outside a thin layer at the entangling surface, as conformal symmetry requires. The region outside the layer sets the universal term of the Rényi entropy. We demonstrate the mechanism in the probe Abelian Higgs model in AdS$_5$ at the Bogomolny point, where the saddles are in closed form and numerical methods confirm the flows. On a sphere we prove that a positive source-free flow exists exactly when $n>n_*$ and the sphere is large enough; it is then unique and ends at the symmetry-breaking defect. The mechanism needs a scalar mass in a finite window, and for potentials attractive at small amplitude, the symmetry can break before conformality is lost. For operators of low enough dimension, already the second Rényi entropy lies beyond the merger. Classical backreaction does not move the merger at any order; we argue that away from $n_*$ the flows persist at first order.

Summing over Seifert Manifolds in Narain Ensemble Averages
Kei Kaneda, Masahito Yamazaki 2611.02515v1 [PDF] Score 5
Entanglement & GeometryQuantum Gravityhep-thgr-qc
The ensemble average of Narain conformal field theories over their moduli space is a real-analytic Eisenstein series, whose Poincaré expansion can be interpreted as a sum over lens spaces weighted by the partition functions of Abelian Chern-Simons theory. In this paper we extend this correspondence from lens spaces to Seifert manifolds, which gives an example of a restricted sum over topologies of the kind that arises in three-dimensional quantum gravity. A Seifert manifold is obtained from a fixed core, a punctured surface times a circle, by gluing solid tori to its boundary tori, and we place a copy of the Narain theory on each of these tori. The ensemble average supplies the sum over the solid tori, or more precisely over marked presentations with fixed base genus, number of fibers and integer Seifert invariant. The resulting sum converges absolutely and is a polynomial in the components of the vector-valued Eisenstein series. When all the boundary tori share a single modulus, the average acquires connected corrections, which we identify with wormholes gluing the core to itself; for two boundary tori, the terms with rational Lagrangian sublattices of the Siegel-Eisenstein series are realized by wormholes containing an exceptional fiber. After reduction along the fiber, the construction closely parallels the two-dimensional topological gravity of Marolf and Maxfield, with the base of the fibration playing the role of their spacetime. We also discuss Dehn fillings of 3-manifolds with torus boundary, non-orientable bases, and the surgery formula. Our results suggest that modularity could be an organizing principle in the summation of geometries in quantum gravity.

We derive an all-orders long-distance expansion of vacuum mutual information between two disjoint balls in generic $d$-dimensional CFTs. The derivation uses a novel radial sewing expansion of the joint vacuum density matrix, together with a modular response formula for relative entropy and the geometric modular flow of a single ball, without analytic continuation in replica number. The expansion coefficients are modular integrals of modular-flowed vacuum correlators. Our formula provides a systematic treatment of arbitrary multi-point sectors, and recovers known results on two-point sector derived from replica methods. The full two-point sector resums into a modular integral of the thermal partition function on $S^{d-1}$, with an effective temperature depending on ball configuration and modular time. Analytic calculations also reproduce known expansion coefficients for 2d free compact boson at self-dual radius and its current sector, as well as for free scalars in higher dimensions.

B. Yesilyurt, N. C. Tsamis, R. P. Woodard 2611.02120v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravitygr-qchep-th
The graviton self-energy $-i [\mbox{}^{μν} Σ^{ρσ}](x;x')$ is the 1PI 2-point function of quantum gravity. It can be used to quantum-correct the linearized Einstein equation in order to infer quantum corrections to gravitational radiation and to the force of gravity. As long as the two points are non-coincident, the graviton self-energy does not need regularization at 1-loop order, however, solving the 1-loop effective field equations requires integrating $[\mbox{}^{μν} Σ^{ρσ}](x;x')$ over ${x'}^μ$, including ${x'}^μ = x^μ$. Quasinormal Modes Criticality across Black Hole Extremality
Zi-Yu Hou, Yu-Qi Lei, Xian-Hui Ge et al. 2611.01869v1 [PDF] Score 3
AdS/CFThep-thgr-qc
We investigate the extremal solution branch to nonextremal branch transition in Einstein-Euler-Heisenberg anti-de Sitter black holes, where the nonlinear electromagnetic parameter $λ$ governs the existence of the extremal branch solution. At fixed pressure and charge, extremal black hole only exist for $λ\leqλ_c$. For $λ>λ_c$, the lowest Hawking temperature keeps nonzero. Near this threshold, the lowest temperature follows a linear critical scaling, $T_{\min}\propto(λ-λ_c)^{α_T}$. We investigate the corresponding dynamical behavior by computing the quasinormal modes of a massless scalar perturbation along the lowest-temperature black hole branch. As the critical threshold is approached, two complex-frequency quasinormal modes branches collide on the imaginary axis and reorganize into distinct purely imaginary branches. Then the imaginary parts of these modes, together with higher overtones, reach zero at the critical threshold, described by $Γ_n\propto(λ-λ_c)^{α_n}$, with $α_n\simeqα_T$. The resulting zero-damped modes become increasingly localized near the event horizon, and their relative damping rates closely follow the overtone spacing predicted by the near-horizon AdS$_2$ spectrum. Our results show that the thermodynamic and dynamical critical behavior remain closely connected even when extremal black hole solutions cease to exist, providing further evidence for the relevance of near-extremal spectral behavior in this distinct critical regime.

\noindent We develop a charge-matched thermodynamic framework for the regular rotating black-hole family underlying an exact model of Kerr formation. Quasi-extremal regular configurations approach, but cannot attain, the entropy bound without losing a regular Killing horizon.

Quantum Recovery from Black-Hole Radiation Beyond the Emission Yield
Kashif Ammar Yasir 2611.01821v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravityquant-phgr-qcphysics.space-ph
A proposed resolution of the black-hole information paradox must explain which information an outside observer can recover from escaping radiation\cite{Hawking,Page,Hayden,Almheiri}. Environmental memory can return correlations to the source without delivering them to that observer\cite{ChenQi,ChenMemory}. Here we establish a sharp recovery frontier at fixed mean radiation yield for single-excitation channels and realize its qubit limit with an autonomous quantum-memory emitter. Unequal transmissions balance retained coherence against information in failed collection events. A reference--radiation correlation test distinguishes this gain even when the mean collected energy is unchanged. Identical transmission channels instead give the same recovery at matched collection, regardless of their emission timescales. For a broader class of interacting sources, an ordering of collected and inaccessible coupling spectra forbids unassisted quantum transmission irrespective of internal memory. A separate coding example supports a positive rate where uniform transmission at matched actual yield has zero capacity. Together, these results identify what openness must change to assist recovery: the accessible transmission structure. They supply a physically constrained comparison of evaporation mechanisms, separating information delivered to radiation from coherence retained elsewhere or an increase in the collected output.
