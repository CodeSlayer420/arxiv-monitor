Defect Anomalies at a Distance
Oleksandr Diatlyk, Zimo Sun, Yifan Wang 2611.08476v1 [PDF] Score 3
CFThep-thcond-mat.str-el
Surface defects carry Weyl and gravitational anomalies that generalize the central charges of two-dimensional conformal field theories (CFTs) and characterize the anomalous response of gapless surface modes to background geometry. These anomalies usually appear as contact terms on the defect. We explain how they are encoded at a distance, in scheme-independent integrated bulk stress-tensor correlators. We derive sum rules for them in terms of defect operator data, establish a lower bound on the boundary Weyl anomaly in three-dimensional CFTs, and illustrate our results with explicit examples.

Accessing Fractional Wilson Lines from U(1) Monodromy Defects on the Fuzzy Sphere
Yin Tang, W. Zhu 2611.08102v1 [PDF] Score 3
CFThep-thcond-mat.stat-mechcond-mat.str-el
Wilson lines are gauge-invariant probes of charge screening and deconfinement, which can detect the response of quantum critical matter to an external charge, but their universal information at strongly-coupled critical points remains hard to access. Here we investigate a continuous family of $U(1)$ monodromy defects of the three-dimensional $O(2)$ Wilson--Fisher conformal field theory, which is dual to fractional Wilson lines in the critical noncompact Abelian Higgs model with one unit-charged scalar. We realize this continuous family of defects within the fuzzy-sphere framework by threading a fractional Aharonov--Bohm flux through the two poles of the sphere. We observe the emergence of charged operators carrying fractional transverse spins, as a characteristic signature of the imposed monodromy. The appearance of displacement operator at its protected dimension supports the conformal nature of this defect line. Crucially, we resolve no relevant symmetry-preserving defect scalar, and identify charge-one monopole operators as relevant symmetry-breaking deformations. Assuming the duality, these results provide non-perturbative evidence that fractional Wilson lines of the critical Abelian Higgs model are stable conformal lines.

Gravitational Thermodynamicization of Primordial Black Holes: An RVB Residue Formulation of Thermal Formation and Nonequilibrium Evolution
Wen-Xiang Chen 2611.07747v1 [PDF] Score 3
Geometric Gravitygr-qc
We establish a controlled, local, and quasi-stationary thermodynamic framework for neutral, nonrotating primordial black holes (PBHs) by combining a local residue prescription with the thermodynamicization of gravitational dynamics. For a nonextremal spherical horizon, we demonstrate that the positive residue of a meromorphic one-form directly determines the inverse Hawking temperature. Applying the first law of thermodynamics recovers the standard Schwarzschild temperature and Bekenstein-Hawking entropy under standard normalization. The resulting entropy defines an off-shell canonical exponent, which reveals that equilibrium thermal production of PBHs is exponentially suppressed in a radiation-dominated universe, thereby confirming that the standard density-perturbation collapse criterion remains the primary formation mechanism. For post-formation evolution, we adopt a detailed-balance mass law, prove the validity of the generalized second law of thermodynamics, and express the evolution dynamics as a free-energy gradient flow where the moving mass acts as an instantaneous evaporation-accretion threshold. Finally, we derive explicit first-order shifts for the temperature, entropy, thermal saddle, and nucleation exponent under small analytic deformations of the residue.
