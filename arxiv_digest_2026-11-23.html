#1
Disk 2-point functions for conformal loop ensemble: a derivation from the SLE loop CFT
Shengzhi Jin, Xin Sun, Baojun Wu 2611.27767v1 [PDF] Score 10
CFTcond-mat.stat-mechhep-thmath-ph
We give a derivation of the bulk two-point functions of two-dimensional critical loop models in the disk based on conformal loop ensembles (CLE) and the conformal field theory of SLE loop measures developed by Baverez--Jego. Combining local Ward identities with the spectral resolution of the SLE-loop CFT, we express the two-point functions as contour integrals involving known CLE three-point structure constants and Virasoro conformal blocks. With a suitable choice of contour, these integrals are absolutely convergent and provide a unified representation of a broad class of correlation functions, valid for both irrational and rational central charges. For irrational central charges, evaluating these integrals by residues determines the discrete closed-channel spectrum and yields expansions that agree with the analytic bootstrap predictions of Downing--Jacobsen--Nivesvivat--Ribault--Saleur.
#2
BMS Charges from Null Hypersurfaces
Martina Adamo, Luca Ciambelli, Felipe Diaz et al. 2611.27488v1 [PDF] Score 6
Asymptotic SymmetriesCelestial Holographyhep-thgr-qc
We show how the gravitational dynamics, phase space and Noether charges of the BMS group at future null infinity $\mathscr{I}^+$ can be derived as limits of their analogues on null hypersurfaces at finite distance. The Bondi mass-loss and angular-momentum evolution equations arise as limits of the Raychaudhuri and Damour constraints. The Ashtekar--Streubel (aka Wald--Zoupas) symplectic potential arises as a specific member of a two-parameter family of null-boundary symplectic potentials, corresponding to the Dirichlet (aka canonical) polarization, while the other members differ by field-space exact terms, leading to the same radiative pre-symplectic structure but potentially different BMS fluxes. Deriving the BMS charges requires additional care, since BMS generators extended into the bulk are generally not tangent to the null hypersurfaces. After accounting for this and the associated anomalies, the renormalized canonical charges still contain an additional soft contribution. The prescription of a covariant boundary Lagrangian determines a corner improvement that removes this term and recovers the standard BMS charges.
#3
Bulk Wilson Loops from $\mathscr{I}$
James Drummond, Bruno Giménez Umbert, Prahar Mitra 2611.27739v1 [PDF] Score 5
Celestial Holographyhep-th
A Carrollian description of four-dimensional Yang-Mills theory on partially complexified future null infinity was recently proposed by Opreij, Skinner and Wang in arXiv:2604.09771. In this paper, we give a new form of the Carrollian Yang-Mills kinetic action on real future null infinity, and recover the logarithmic two-point function found by arXiv:2604.09771. We then extend the Carrollian description of Yang-Mills beyond scattering amplitudes by studying the expectation value of a null polygonal Wilson loop in Minkowski spacetime. We propose a boundary operator given by the trace of a cyclic product of holomorphic frames on the good cuts associated with the polygon's vertices. At order $g^2$, we show analytically that the boundary calculation reproduces the rational bulk integrand obtained from a self-dual Lagrangian insertion.
#4
Polarized IKKT holography: bulk realization of BPS operators
Franz Ciceri, Aymeric Proust, Henning Samtleben 2611.27482v1 [PDF] Score 5
AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-th
We study the holographic imprint of polarization in the lowest BPS multiplet of the IKKT matrix model. On the matrix model side, the deformation requires operator mixing and broken-symmetry Ward identities to organize the surviving operators into an irreducible atypical multiplet of the $F(4)$ superalgebra. In the bulk, we use one-dimensional maximal gauged supergravity to identify the analytic half-BPS background with $F(4)$ symmetry whose boundary behavior matches the IKKT polarization. We solve the linearized bosonic and fermionic equations around this background and its undeformed counterpart exactly, and extract the independent asymptotic data. The supersymmetry transformations of these data reproduce the matrix model multiplets, thus establishing a bulk realization of this BPS operator structure.
#5
The moments and asymptotic spectral distribution of the Grassmannian ensemble
Aryan Gadhave, Asghar Ghorbanpour, Liam Hickey et al. 2611.27914v1 [PDF] Score 3
Entanglement & Geometryquant-phmath-phmath.PR
In this paper, we study random density matrices constructed by taking partial traces of projections onto random subspaces of a bipartite Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_{B}$ distributed according to the pushforward measure of the action of the unitary group on the Grassmannian $\text{Gr}_{n}(\mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_{B})$. This ensemble models a `typical' quantum subspace. All tracial moments of the density operator, as well as mixed tracial moments of reduced density operators, are computed explicitly using Weingarten calculus. There are several consequences of these results. In particular, we prove that the empirical spectral distribution of the density operator converges almost surely to a rescaled Marchenko-Pastur law. We also prove that this ensemble can be written as a sum of asymptotically free random density operators, leading to an alternative proof of the convergence of the spectral distribution. Finally, we compute the asymptotic expansion of the average entanglement entropy.
#6
Detection of genuine multipartite entanglement by means of entanglement monotones
Sylvain Carrozza, Johann Chevrier, Luca Lionni 2611.27660v1 [PDF] Score 3
Entanglement & Geometryquant-phmath-ph
We look for pure state entanglement monotones that may serve as certificates of a strong form of genuine multipartite entanglement, that we refer to as irreducible entanglement: a $D$-partite state (with $D\geq 3$) is irreducibly $D$-partite entangled if it cannot be decomposed as a product of states that are at most $(D-1)$-partite entangled. We start by establishing a no-go result proving that no reasonable genuine multipartite entanglement entropy can simultaneously obey LOCC-monotonicity and subadditivity. After relaxing the first condition to LO-monotonicity, we provide a simple method generating genuine multipartite entanglement monotones with the desired properties. Focusing on a subfamily of these monotones, we show that they are able to detect any form of genuine tripartite entanglement in three-qubit systems.
