On Entanglement Dynamics in Holographic Theories
Hanzhi Jiang 2611.07010v1 [PDF] Score 19
AdS/CFTCFTEntanglement & Geometryhep-thcond-mat.stat-mechcond-mat.str-el
Entanglement entropy (EE) is central to modern theoretical physics, yet its calculation in interacting theories with nontrivial time dependence remains challenging. In this thesis, we investigate the real-time dynamics of EE in strongly coupled quantum field theories (QFTs) using the Anti-de Sitter/Conformal Field Theory (AdS/CFT) correspondence. We first examine EE evolution in an exactly solvable two-dimensional conformal field theory (CFT) with an inhomogeneous sine-square deformation (SSD). We argue that the unconventional time evolution driven by the SSD Hamiltonian can be viewed as a change in spacetime foliation, producing new horizons in the dual three-dimensional bulk. Holographic EE calculations agree with CFT results in all comparable cases. We then study entanglement dynamics in generic chaotic quantum many-body systems. In the limit of late time and large regions, these dynamics are believed to admit an effective entanglement membrane description. In 2d CFTs, a degenerate membrane tension reproduces several entropy dynamics results, unlike the non-degenerate tension in higher dimensions. Using holography, we show that capturing reflected entropy in 2d CFT requires an additional degree of freedom in the membrane description. We further show that a conventional non-degenerate tension emerges when the CFT undergoes a relevant deformation, holographically dual to a planar Banados-Teitelboim-Zanelli (BTZ) black hole with scalar hair and an interior Kasner universe. We then develop a membrane effective theory for reflected entropy dynamics in higher dimensions. Finally, we apply the membrane theories for reflected entropy to study the time evolution of multipartite entanglement signals. We found that scrambling dynamics generate multipartite entanglement with rich structures.

Matrix Theory from Holography II: Beyond Counting
Shota Komatsu, Eunwoo Lee, Chintan Patel 2611.06999v1 [PDF] Score 17
AdS/CFTCFTString Theoryhep-th
Extending our earlier realization of Matrix Theory for M-theory within AdS/CFT, we propose and test a new triality among a large-charge sector of three-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ ADHM theory, the BMN matrix model, and lightlike-compactified M-theory on the maximally supersymmetric pp-wave. In an appropriate triple-scaling limit, the superconformal index of ADHM theory becomes the grand-canonical index of the BMN model, with magnetic-flux sectors corresponding to fuzzy-sphere vacua. But the real interest of this new setup is that we can go beyond counting states. Using supersymmetric localization, we show that correlation functions in topological subsectors agree as well, including their nontrivial dependence on the BMN coupling. In each magnetic-flux sector, both the localization measure and the operator insertions can be matched precisely with those of the corresponding BMN vacuum. Our results also point to a deeper connection between monopole bubbling in ADHM theory and instantons in the BMN model. For minuscule monopole charges, ADHM correlators map directly to topological correlators in the trivial BMN vacuum. For non-minuscule charges, monopole bubbling produces additional screened sectors, whose relative weights reproduce the classical actions of BMN instantons. Mirror symmetry offers another perspective on this structure, mapping the monopole operators to Higgs-branch ``winding" operators in the mirror necklace theory. Finally, we discuss a type IIB counterpart, connecting a large-charge sector of four-dimensional $\mathbb Z_K$-orbifold quiver theories, the DLCQ of type IIB string theory on the pp-wave, and level-one mass-deformed ABJM theory on $\mathbb R_t\times T^2$. As a first test, we show that the superconformal index of the orbifold theory reproduces the vacuum degeneracy of the mass-deformed ABJM theory.

Conformal field theory from automorphic forms on a quantum group
Dalimil Mazac 2611.06907v1 [PDF] Score 11
CFThep-thmath-phmath.NT
We reformulate two-dimensional conformal field theory (2d CFT) in the language of noncommutative geometry. To this end, we introduce automorphic forms for the von Neumann algebraic quantum group $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$. A quantum measure space is a von Neumann algebra equipped with a weight. We study such spaces carrying a weight-preserving action of $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$. We argue that every unitary bosonic 2d CFT with central charge $c=1+6(b+b^{-1})^2>1$ is equivalent to a quantum measure space with an action of $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})\times\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$. In this dictionary, Virasoro primary operators are quantum automorphic forms and their scaling dimensions correspond to the spectrum of a quantum Laplace operator. The Virasoro crossing equations express associativity of multiplication on the quantum measure space. For compact theories, the algebra is a type III$_1$ factor. Liouville CFT is realized as $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$ acting on itself by left and right translations. Virasoro boundary conditions correspond to quantum measure spaces with an action of a single copy of $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$. When the boundary spectrum is discrete, these are noncommutative counterparts of compact hyperbolic surfaces. The formulation accommodates models both with and without extended chiral algebras. It bridges the Virasoro conformal bootstrap with categorical and von Neumann algebraic approaches to 2d CFT.

Form Factors in AdS from the Worldsheet
Luis F. Alday, Clément Virally, Xinan Zhou 2611.06679v1 [PDF] Score 9
AdS/CFTCFTString Theoryhep-th
We study AdS form factors describing closed strings scattering off D-branes. Assuming that these form factors admit worldsheet integral representations in terms of multiple polylogarithms, we determine their leading curvature corrections around the flat-space limit by matching their pole structure to CFT data. We focus on two examples of defects in 4d $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM: a $\frac{1}{2}$-BPS 't Hooft loop, dual to a D1 brane, and a pair of giant gravitons, dual to a D3 brane. For the 't Hooft loop, the worldsheet bootstrap completely fixes the form factor, and the result passes nontrivial checks from supersymmetric localization. For the giant gravitons, the bootstrap initially leaves five free coefficients, one of which can be fixed using localization. Remarkably, imposing instead that no new operators appear in the defect channel at this order fixes all five coefficients and reproduces the localization constraint. Under this additional assumption, we therefore obtain a unique D3-brane form factor, with supersymmetric localization providing an independent check.

We develop a novel conformal bootstrap formulated directly in terms of conformal symmetry, reflection positivity, and crossing symmetry, without explicitly relying on the operator product expansion or conformal blocks. By constructing Gram matrices from states created by pairs of local operators and imposing spectral constraints through conformal generators, we obtain a hierarchy of semidefinite programs that directly constrain correlation functions. Zoltan Bajnok, Romuald A. Janik 2611.06473v1 [PDF] Score 7
AdS/CFTCFTEntanglement & Geometryhep-thcond-mat.stat-mechquant-ph
We propose a Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz (TBA) approach for describing thermalization in integrable quantum field theories. The exponential damping and oscillations when approaching thermal equilibrium can be understood as the analogs of quasi-normal mode (QNM) frequencies of black hole perturbations within the framework of holography. Holographic intuitions and some general properties of thermal Green's functions motivate the existence of such a TBA formulation, as well as lead to the imposition of a nonstandard analytical structure on the QNM TBA, while comparison with integrable form-factor expansions fixes the remaining ingredients. We conjecture the QNM TBA to be equivalent to performing an all-order resummation of secular terms in a thermal form factor expansion of retarded 2-point correlation functions. We compare the predictions with tensor network simulations of the E8 deformation of Ising CFT at nonzero temperature. We conjecture that the QNM TBA framework should also be applicable for describing out-of-equilibrium approach to Generalized Hydrodynamics (GHD).

Spectral flow in AdS$\mathbf{_3}$ revisited
Nathan McStay, Ronald Reid-Edwards, Sean Seet 2611.06295v2 [PDF] Score 7
AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-th
We revisit an old idea that spectral flow in WZW models can be associated with certain $G$-bundles over a worldsheet. This allows us to introduce a novel method for computing correlators of spectrally-flowed vertex operators in WZW models with group $G$ and level $k$, by systematically computing the contribution from a network of line defects. Specialising to the case of $G=SL(2, \mathbb{R})$, as is relevant to AdS$_3$ string theory, we demonstrate that generic $n$-point functions of spectrally-flowed vertex operators at tree level can be rewritten in terms of an $(n+1)$-point function of $n$ unflowed vertex operators and one degenerate insertion. We verify that our results agree with bootstrap results in the literature. This provides the next step towards fully solving the $SL(2,\mathbb{R})$ WZW model.

Tidal Love Numbers of Thin-Shell Wormholes, de Sitter Gravastars, and Anti-de Sitter Gravastars via Full Perturbed Junction Conditions
Gerui Chen, Yu Tian, Hongbao Zhang 2611.07341v1 [PDF] Score 7
AdS/CFTEntanglement & Geometrygr-qc
In this work, we revisit the tidal Love numbers (TLNs) of several classes of exotic compact objects (ECOs) using the recently derived full perturbed junction conditions. We investigate four representative thin-shell models: wormholes, de Sitter gravastars with zero surface energy density, general de Sitter gravastars, and anti-de Sitter gravastars. Previous studies of wormholes and de Sitter gravastars with zero surface energy density, based on simplified matching conditions, suggested that the TLNs of ultracompact ECOs vanish logarithmically in the black-hole (BH) limit, leading to the conjecture that this logarithmic dependence is a generic feature of ultracompact exotic objects. We show that this logarithmic behavior is not universal once the full perturbed junction conditions are imposed. In particular, the odd-parity TLNs exhibit polynomial scaling in the BH limit for all the models considered, while the even-parity sector displays both logarithmic and polynomial behaviors depending on the model. We also find that the signs of the TLNs can differ qualitatively from previous results, especially in the odd-parity sector. Our results demonstrate that the tidal response of ECOs is strongly affected by the perturbed matching conditions and that the previously conjectured universal logarithmic scaling near the BH limit is not generic.

Bruno Toniato, David Dudal, Hudson Almeida et al. 2611.06411v1 [PDF] Score 5
AdS/CFTGeometric Gravityhep-th
A fully dynamical Einstein-Proca-dilaton (EPD) holographic model is developed for the exotic $J^{PC}=1^{-+}$ hybrid meson, incorporating the backreaction of the massive vector field dual to the hybrid current in the bulk. Solving the coupled EPD system yields a self-consistent gravitational background and the mass spectrum of hybrid resonances. While the soft-wall limit reproduces linear Regge behavior, the inclusion of backreaction yields quantitative shifts that improve agreement with experimental candidates. Recently, Takaura proposed a new method for extracting the first nonzero coefficient of the low-energy expansion of an observable from its operator product expansion (OPE) in asymptotically free theories with a mass gap and a single threshold. The method uses a weight function to suppress the contribution of the ``duality-violation'' (DV) term that arises when relating the OPE to the low-energy expansion. We study how boundary correlation functions encode the charge dependence of the renormalised Euclidean action of an AdS$_3$ black brane. In three-dimensional Einstein-Maxwell theory, the Maxwell field has logarithmic asymptotics and is locally dual to a massless pseudoscalar. In the canonical ensemble, the charge density fixes the slope of a spatially linear source for the dual marginal operator. At fixed temperature, the classical action is even in the charge density due to reflection symmetry $Φ\to-Φ$, and its charge expansion coefficients are governed by Catalan numbers. We compute the quadratic and quartic terms from connected correlation functions around the neutral brane. The scalar two-point function and the shift symmetry of its source determine the matter Weyl anomaly, which fixes the logarithmic temperature dependence of the quadratic term in the charge expansion. At quartic order, the connected four-point function comes from tree-level graviton exchange. At each higher order, the corresponding Catalan coefficient predicts the integrated sum of all connected tree-level Witten diagrams.

Demystifying Graviton Detection
Igor Pikovski 2611.07447v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravitygr-qchep-thquant-ph
Detecting single gravitons is possible through the extraction of discrete energy quanta from passing gravitational waves. For decades, this was considered hopeless, with estimates suggesting a detector the size of Jupiter. Here, I explain how these earlier estimates relied on the highly inefficient electron ionization process, while the absorption through the full mass quadrupole of a macroscopic system can bring graviton detection within experimental reach. Operating in the LIGO frequency band solves the challenges of graviton sources and noise suppression, allowing gravitons to be extracted from known passing gravitational waves and detection events to be heralded by classical gravitational-wave observations. Combined with the ability to resolve single energy quanta in macroscopic systems, such as in quantum acoustics, this opens a realistic path toward graviton detection. I also discuss the general limitations of particle detection in establishing an underlying quantum theory, illustrated by an energy-nonconserving semiclassical alternative. Nevertheless, the semiclassical alternative is not standard classical wave physics, and extracting $E=\hbarω$ from a passing wave constitutes bona fide particle detection. This opens the way not only to the first detection of individual gravitons, but also to experimentally probing linearized quantum gravity and the graviton counting statistics of gravitational waves.

Finite is not enough: Triviality bounds in nonlocal field theories
Fabian Wagner 2611.07026v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravityhep-thgr-qchep-ph
An ultraviolet-finite theory need not be ultraviolet complete. This observation weakens the case for spacetime discreteness in quantum gravity if that case rests on removing ultraviolet divergences alone. We arrive at this observation by deriving the one-loop momentum-subtraction flow in four-dimensional $φ^4$ theories with ultraviolet-suppressing form factors. The suppression freezes the running above the nonlocality scale, but the theory can hit a Landau pole before suppression sets in. We obtain a bound on the infrared coupling by requiring perturbative control at all scales. This bound varies only at the percent level across the examples we consider. We expect similar bounds in models of discrete spacetime. Thus, requiring ultraviolet completeness improves the predictivity of nonlocal theories.

Asymptotic safety is not generic
Francesco Del Porro, Francesco Ferrarin, Alessia Platania 2611.07002v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravityhep-thgr-qc
Asymptotically safe quantum gravity proposes a non-perturbative ultraviolet completion of gravity in which the high-energy behavior is controlled by an ultraviolet fixed point---the Reuter fixed point---for which there is robust evidence from a wide range of settings and truncations. The existence of asymptotic safety requires non-trivial zeros of beta functions, raising the question of how generic such fixed-point solutions are. In this work, we tackle this issue by investigating chiral perturbation theories. We focus on SU(2) and SU(3) chiral models, which share key features with gravity such as perturbative non-renormalizability or nonlinear interactions, but which are known to admit a different ultraviolet completion via new degrees of freedom. Using the functional renormalization group, we study these theories within a quartic-derivative truncation and find no evidence for an interacting fixed point. This result suggests that asymptotic safety is not generic, but rather a feature pertaining to certain special systems.

The Kogut-Susskind (KS) formulation of gauge theories constructs quantum many-body Hamiltonians with gauge invariance under a compact Lie group exactly implemented on a spatial lattice. Here, we revisit this construction for a non-compact gauge group. Although motivated in part by theories like general relativity whose first-order formulation involves the non-compact Lorentz group as a local symmetry, we focus on simpler cases in one spatial dimension akin to the Schwinger model. In a bottom-up approach, we show how exact gauge invariance under the simplest non-compact Lie group ${\rm SL}(2,\mathbb{R})$ can be retained exactly on a 1D lattice, and highlight the differences that arise with respect to compact gauge groups, including a continuous spectrum of the Casimir operators of the gauge fields and the emergence of infinite-dimensional representations of the matter fields. Wen-Xiang Chen 2611.07747v1 [PDF] Score 3

We establish a controlled, local, and quasi-stationary thermodynamic framework for neutral, nonrotating primordial black holes (PBHs) by combining a local residue prescription with the thermodynamicization of gravitational dynamics. For a nonextremal spherical horizon, we demonstrate that the positive residue of a meromorphic one-form directly determines the inverse Hawking temperature. Applying the first law of thermodynamics recovers the standard Schwarzschild temperature and Bekenstein-Hawking entropy under standard normalization. The resulting entropy defines an off-shell canonical exponent, which reveals that equilibrium thermal production of PBHs is exponentially suppressed in a radiation-dominated universe, thereby confirming that the standard density-perturbation collapse criterion remains the primary formation mechanism. Constraining Lorentz Invariance Violation via Precision Atomic Spectroscopy
A. S. Lemos, J. E. J. Matias, F. Dahia et al. 2611.06392v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravitygr-qcquant-ph
This paper investigates Lorentz violation arising from quantum gravity effects in electronic and muonic hydrogen atoms. The violation is modeled through a modified dispersion relation parameterized by a dimensionless coefficient $ξ$ and suppressed by the Planck scale. Using high-precision spectroscopic data and a chi-squared fitting procedure that carefully accounts for theoretical uncertainties in the Rydberg constant and the proton radius, the analysis yields a 95% confidence level constraint $ξ\in [-14.3,\; 1.37] \times 10^5$. The mass scaling of the effect reveals two distinct regimes: for the lowest-order deformation, the heavy nucleus amplifies the signal, while for higher-order corrections, the lepton dominates the sensitivity. These bounds approach Planck-scale sensitivity using the simplest atomic systems, thereby circumventing ambiguities inherent in more complex quantum systems.
