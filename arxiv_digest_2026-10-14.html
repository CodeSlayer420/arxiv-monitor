Modular Flow and Holographic Timelike Entanglement Entropy in AdS and Flat Holography
Mehdi Hakami Shalamzari, Zahra Paygozar, Mahdi Kord Zangeneh 2610.17165v1 [PDF] Score 25
AdS/CFTCFTCelestial HolographyEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
Timelike entanglement entropy provides a useful probe of holographic theoris, yet an explicit bulk realization of the modular flow underlying its timelike extremal curve construction has so far been lacking. We construct modular flows associated with spacelike and timelike intervals in AdS$_{3}$/CFT$_{2}$ and extend this framework to flat$_{3}$/CCFT$_{2}$. Beginning with the boundary symmetry generators, we explicitly derive the boundary modular flows and extend them into the bulk, providing a systematic procedure that relates bulk modular flows to the extremal curves and holographic (timelike) entanglement entropy. For spacelike intervals, the resulting bulk modular flows determine the extremal curves, whose integrated line elements reproduce the standard holographic entanglement entropy. We then generalize this approach to timelike intervals, explicitly constructing the modular flows whose fixed points reproduce the extremal spacelike and timelike curves which gives the holographic timelike entanglement entropy. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first explicit construction of the modular flow associated with a timelike extremal curve for a timelike interval. The same procedure is then carried over to flat$_{3}$/CCFT$_{2}$, which give rise to the holographic (timelike) entanglement entropy. Finally, we show that the flat space results obtained in this work can be recovered from their AdS$_3$ counterparts in the flat space limit.

Holographic Pseudo Entropy from the Rindler Method in AdS and Flat Space
Mehdi Hakami Shalamzari, Zahra Paygozar, Mahdi Kord Zangeneh 2610.17239v1 [PDF] Score 18
AdS/CFTCFTCelestial HolographyEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
Via the Rindler method, we study holographic timelike entanglement entropy (TEE) for a general timelike interval in AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ and flat$_3$/CCFT$_2$. The TEE is the pseudo entropy of a timelike interval. In the bulk, the imaginary part of the TEE comes from a timelike geodesic, but in the Rindler picture this geodesic has not been identified. After a Rindler transformation to a rotating BTZ black hole, the two spacelike extremal curves lie on the inner and outer horizons, and exchanging $l_u\leftrightarrow \ell l_φ$ takes one to the other. We find the geometric meaning of the timelike extremal curve: a geodesic stretched between the two horizons. Its length, $iπ\ell$, is independent of the interval, and it alone produces the imaginary part $iπc/6$. We follow the same procedure in flat space holography, where the spacelike curve sits on the cosmological horizon and the timelike curve lies between the cosmological horizon and timelike infinity. Evaluating the holographic TEE for a timelike interval in CCFT$_2$ on the plane, we find that, in contrast with the cylinder, the real and imaginary parts are equal.

Area metric gravity à la Plebanski: connection, curvature and dynamics
Bianca Dittrich 2610.15838v1 [PDF] Score 12
AdS/CFTQuantum GravityString Theoryhep-th
Area metrics arise as effective description at intermediate scales in a number of approaches to quantum gravity, including string theory, holography and loop quantum gravity. Recently, it has been shown that area metric actions can be derived from the modified Plebanski framework on the linearized level. Surprisingly these actions avoid extra poles in the propagator for the effective length dynamics, and hence constitute interesting deformations of linearized Einstein gravity. To be able to study whether this property persist for the full theory, we construct here non-linear area metric dynamics from modified Plebanski actions. We provide the geometric tools for area metrics that do not rely on perturbation theory and allow a systematic construction and study of general non-linear area metric actions: an algorithm that extracts a length metric from an area metric and a connection on two-forms that is uniquely determined by the area metric, together with its curvature and curvature invariants. We derive area metric actions from modified Plebanski actions in first and second order versions. We also find that area metric actions can be reformulated as Poincare gauge theories. We determine the effective length metric action, which on a flat background describes a graviton and a massive spin-two field with parity-violating couplings set by the Barbero--Immirzi parameter. Additionally we discuss cosmological backgrounds and the role of the matter coupling, which together open the way to cosmological applications.

Geometry and holography of $\mathrm{AdS}_2\times\mathrm{S}^2$: towards the field theory dual
Miroslava Mosso Rojas, Anayeli Ramirez 2610.18344v1 [PDF] Score 8
AdS/CFTCFTString Theoryhep-th
We take a first step in identifying the superconformal quantum mechanics dual to supersymmetric AdS$_2\times$S$^2$ solutions. We focus on the Type IIB solutions of the form AdS$_2\times$S$^2\times$CY$_2\timesΣ$ that preserve eight supercharges, with $Σ$ a two-dimensional space parametrised by $(x_1,x_2)$, and read off their brane content, a D1-D5-NS5 intersection with D3 and D7 flavour branes. We propose that their dual $\mathcal{N}=4$ quantum mechanics is organised over a two-dimensional lattice of cells in $Σ$. Namely, it consists of copies of the linear quiver along $x_1$ dual to the U(1)-invariant solutions of the class, one for each interval along $x_2$, with ranks given by the D1 and D5 charges of the corresponding cell. Its central charge agrees with the holographic one at leading order. We show that the family of SL$(2,\mathbb{R})$ non-Abelian T-duals of the AdS$_3\times$S$^2\times$CY$_2$ solutions of massive Type IIA supergravity, constructed in previous work, belongs to this class, as does the SL$(2,\mathbb{R})\times$SU$(2)$ non-Abelian T-dual of AdS$_3\times$S$^3\times$CY$_2$, and work both out in detail. For both we propose global completions that introduce additional D3 and D7 branes. Along $x_2$ we interpret the fundamental strings stretched between the colour branes and the additional D3 and D7 branes as baryon vertices. Finally, we extend this proposal from the non-Abelian T-duals to the solutions of the class whose defining functions factorise in $x_1$ and $x_2$, including those in which the AdS$_2$ and S$^2$ factors have the same warp factor.

Pseudo-spectral methods in gravitational lensing: Spin precession in loop quantum black hole spacetimes
Xiankai Pang, Xiao-Kan Guo 2610.18064v1 [PDF] Score 7
Quantum Gravitygr-qc
Gravitational lensing is usually treated as an initial value problem: for a given impact parameter of an unbound orbit, one computes the resulting deflection angle. In this paper we advocate the inverse point of view. For a prescribed deflection angle, the orbit is selected by two boundary conditions at spatial infinity, so that the lensing problem becomes a well-defined two-point boundary value problem, with the impact parameter playing the role of a nonlinear eigenvalue. We show that pseudo-spectral methods based on Chebyshev collocation solve this boundary value problem accurately at modest computational cost, and that the parallel transport equations governing the spin precession of the particle reduce to a single linear system on the same grid. As a concrete application, we compute the spin precession of massive particles in backward scattering off two black hole spacetimes from loop quantum gravity: the covariant effective metric and the self-dual loop quantum black hole. All equations are written for a general static, spherically symmetric line element with three free functions, so that both spacetimes are handled on the same footing. We find that the quantum corrections are most pronounced for slowly moving particles, while in the ultra-relativistic limit the universal spin flip is always recovered. The flip itself is universal, but the rate at which it is approached is not: the derivative of $\cosξ$ with respect to the particle speed at $v=1$ is a strong-field quantity in every backward channel and depends clearly on the quantum parameters of both spacetimes. The framework is not restricted to the models studied

Evading the Swampland de Sitter Obstruction via Thermodynamic Duality-Induced Horizon Corrections in Unimodular Gravity
Reza Baghbani 2610.17438v1 [PDF] Score 6
Geometric GravityQuantum Gravityhep-thgr-qc
We investigate the early-universe cosmological implications of Unimodular Gravity coupled to Power-Law Nonlinear Electrodynamics in the presence of quantum-corrected horizon thermodynamics derived from the principle of thermodynamic duality. By incorporating both logarithmic and inverse-area corrections to the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, we derive modified Friedmann equations exhibiting novel $H^4$ and $H^6$ terms that represent genuine quantum gravity effects. We demonstrate that these higher-order corrections become dynamically significant in the high-energy regime of cosmic inflation, naturally modifying the slow-roll dynamics without requiring a fundamental inflaton potential. A comprehensive phase-space analysis reveals that the quantum corrections drive early-time de Sitter-like attractors, providing a robust mechanism for sustained inflationary expansion. Confronting the model with the Swampland program, we show that the correction factor $\mathcal{F}(H)$ modifies the effective slow-roll parameters, enabling the model to satisfy the Swampland de Sitter and Distance conjectures during the inflationary epoch. We calculate the inflationary observables, including the spectral index $n_s$ and the tensor-to-scalar ratio $r$, and demonstrate that the model's predictions are in excellent agreement with the Planck 2018 observational constraints. Our results suggest that thermodynamic duality-induced horizon corrections offer a viable and testable pathway for realizing successful inflation within a well-motivated quantum-gravitational framework.

Imaginary HTEE: Probing Criticality and Coherence
M. Ali-Akbari, M. M. Daryaei Goki 2610.18716v1 [PDF] Score 5
AdS/CFTEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
We study the imaginary part of the holographic timelike entanglement entropy (HTEE) in a charged asymptotically AdS$_5$ black-hole background whose dual field theory exhibits a critical point in the presence of a chemical potential $μ$ and temperature $T$. Identifying the imaginary part of the HTEE with the imaginary part of the pseudoentropy, we derive an analytical expression for this quantity and investigate its behavior on the stable and unstable thermodynamic branches. We find that the imaginary HTEE vanishes in the uncharged limit and is induced entirely by a nonzero chemical potential, signaling the emergence of quantum coherence between the initial and final states. As the dimensionless ratio $μ/T$ approaches its critical value, we obtain an inverse-square-root divergence in the derivative of the imaginary HTEE and extract the critical exponent $θ_I = 1/2$ analytically. We further show that the same exponent governs the temperature response at fixed chemical potential. Our results establish the imaginary part of the HTEE as a non-local quantum-information probe of criticality in strongly coupled systems, and they imply that the initial and final states become maximally coherent at the critical point.

Optical Signatures of a Black Hole in Effective Quantum Gravity
Jonibek Khasanov, Xuwei Zhang, Mirzabek Alloqulov et al. 2610.16721v1 [PDF] Score 5
Quantum Gravitygr-qc
In this article, we present an investigation of observational signatures of a black hole (BH) in effective quantum gravity (EQG), particularly gravitational weak lensing and shadow analyses with the assumption that the BH is surrounded by uniform and non-uniform plasma. First, we review the spacetime by comparing it with well-known BH spacetimes such as Schwarzschild and Reissner--Nordström BHs. Subsequently, we explore the effect of the uniform and non-uniform plasma on the BH shadow. The results indicate that shadow radii decrease under the influence of the BH charge and both uniform and non-uniform plasma parameters. In addition, we obtain theoretical constraints on the spacetime parameters using observational data from the Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) collaboration. Finally, we study gravitational weak lensing around the BH in EQG surrounded by uniform and non-uniform plasma. It was found that increasing the uniform plasma frequency leads to a rise in the deflection angle and vice versa for the non-uniform plasma.

Carrollian Yang-Mills theories in the homogeneous temporal gauge
Carmelo P. Martin 2610.18715v1 [PDF] Score 4
Celestial Holographyhep-th
We quantize, in the homogeneous temporal gauge, both the electric and the magnetic Carrollian Yang-Mills theories with SU(N) gauge symmetry in the fundamental representation. We use the BRST method implemented in the path integral. We show that in the first case all the interaction vertices vanish, thus, giving a free theory. In the magnetic case, there are no loop contributions to the Green functions of the elementary fields --i.e., Green functions not involving composite operators-- and the only connected parts of such Green functions with a dependence on the coupling constant are the tree level contributions to the three and four point functions of the field $ξ_i$. We also show that in the magnetic case the Green functions involving only Wilson loops are trivial and not so in the electric case.

Ziyi Li 2610.18591v1 [PDF] Score 4
AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-th
We study aspects of the Euclidean partition function of the $\frac{SL(2,\mathbb R)\times U(1)}{U(1)}$ gauged WZW model. The target space geometry is either a smooth horizonless geometry with a linear-dilaton asymptotic region, or the Horne--Horowitz black string, depending on which circles we unwind as the time-direction. We first study the worldsheet spectrum and obtain the character decomposition via the holonomy integrals. Next, we obtain the spacetime spectrum of the horizonless geometry by directly performing the unfolded modular integrals, and recover the Maldacena-Ooguri-Son spectrum in the ungauged limit. When the chirally charged three-dimensional black string is dimensionally reduced, the target space is instead a two-dimensional charged black hole. We study the near extremal regime of the black hole and black string. For the two dimensional black hole, we propose two candidate towers of states that produce the expected $\frac{3}{2}\log T$ of the AdS$_2$ throat from the worldsheet discrete series representation. These towers appear only below the temperature at which the full amplitude becomes divergent. We comment on the contribution of these states in the near-extremal limit of the full partition function.

Asymptotic analysis of the confluent Dotsenko-Fateev system
Shunya Adachi 2610.16970v1 [PDF] Score 3
CFTmath.CAmath-ph
The Dotsenko-Fateev equation is a third-order linear ordinary differential equation studied in two-dimensional conformal field theory. We study the confluent Dotsenko-Fateev system, a rank-three system of linear ordinary differential equations obtained by confluence from a first-order system equivalent to the Dotsenko-Fateev equation. It has an irregular singular point at infinity, and its solutions admit double integral representations of confluent Selberg type. Using these representations, we construct sectorial fundamental solution matrices around infinity, establish their asymptotic expansions, and determine the associated Stokes matrices explicitly. We further study three aspects of the confluent Dotsenko-Fateev system: its equivalence to the degree-two polynomial reduction of the Schrödinger equation for the quantum Painlevé equation of type V, its realization as a restriction of a confluent version of the linear Pfaffian system associated with Appell's hypergeometric function $F_4$, and its reduction to the trivial rank-one equation via integral transforms and a quadratic change of the independent variable.
