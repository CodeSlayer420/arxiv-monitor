#1
Massive Particles in Carrollian Holography
Bin Chen, Zezhou Hu, Reiko Liu et al. 2611.28780v1 [PDF] Score 9
AdS/CFTCFTCelestial Holographyhep-th
Understanding how massive particles are encoded in boundary operators is a central problem in Carrollian holography. We construct a three-dimensional Carrollian description of a massive scalar in four-dimensional flat spacetime, together with an explicit bulk-to-boundary integral transform. In this construction, nonzero mass requires infinitely many linearly independent primary states, while the absence of bulk spin imposes relations among their descendants. We show that these relations are equivalent to null-state decoupling and give a local constraint on the boundary fields. The integral transform preserves the full Poincaré symmetry and maps bulk scalar data to fields satisfying this constraint. We also obtain two- and three-point functions and the associated formal operator product expansion coefficients. The integral transform evaluates bulk data at complex momenta, making analytic continuation essential in Lorentzian signature. This construction provides a concrete starting point for relating massive scattering amplitudes to Carrollian correlators and for reconstructing bulk fields from boundary data.

Scattering amplitudes in AdS$_5\times$ S$^5$ from the pure spinor superstring
Luis F. Alday, Thiago Fleury, Tobias Hansen et al. 2611.29622v1 [PDF] Score 8
AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-th
We formulate a BRST-invariant prescription for the integration of zero modes in the pure spinor superstring on $AdS_5\times S^5$. The measure includes contributions absent in flat space and admits a description in terms of picture changing operators or a non-minimal regulator. The prescription is valid for genus-zero amplitudes with arbitrary multiplicity and external states and removes a central obstacle to amplitude computations in this background. We use it to perform the first direct worldsheet computation of two and three-point amplitudes of half-BPS supergravity states. The properly normalised three-point amplitudes exactly reproduce the results of planar ${\mathcal N}=4$ super Yang-Mills theory.

A note on Holographic Renormalization for Polarized IKKT
Matteo Guglielmetti 2611.29677v1 [PDF] Score 6
AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-th
We construct a boundary counterterm for the on-shell action of type IIB supergravity solutions dual to vacua of the polarized IKKT matrix model. The construction is based on the variational principle, with off-shell fluctuations whose fall-off conditions are based on the asymptotic equations of motion. The renormalized on-shell action of the supersymmetric saddles reproduces the matrix- model result once we pass to the ensemble of fixed D1-brane charges, which we implement off-shell by dualizing the RR two-form potential.

Dirichlet walls beyond JT gravity and their quantum group interpretation
Luca Griguolo, Jacopo Papalini, Lorenzo Russo et al. 2611.30567v1 [PDF] Score 5
AdS/CFTQuantum Gravityhep-th
We canonically quantize sinh dilaton gravity with finite Dirichlet boundary conditions. The reduced phase space is formulated in terms of the length of a two-sided spacelike geodesic in an effective AdS$_2$ geometry and its conjugate momentum. Canonical quantization leads to a finite-difference Schrödinger equation whose wavefunctions of the universe are given by $q$-hypergeometric functions. These furnish a quantum deformation of the hypergeometric wavefunctions of JT gravity. We further show that the finite cutoff Hamiltonian derived from the bulk analysis admits a natural interpretation as the reduced Casimir of $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R}))$ acting on hyperbolic matrix elements. The two scattering wave solutions are hyperbolic matrix elements of the modular double $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb R))\otimes U_{\tilde q}(\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb R))$, and the wavefunction of the universe is their unique combination regular at vanishing length.
#6
Exact stress-tensor fluctuations from conformal symmetry and black hole radiation
José Navarro-Salas, Roberto Balbinot, Alessandro Fabbri 2611.29060v1 [PDF] Score 5
CFTQuantum Gravityhep-th
Semiclassical gravity is sourced by the expectation value of the stress tensor, and it is reliable only if the fluctuations around this value are small. In two dimensions, conformal symmetry captures the essential physics of Hawking radiation in its s-wave sector and determines the mean value exactly, for any background and any quantum state. We show that it also determines the local fluctuations of the null components $T_{\pm\pm}$. The square of the stress tensor is defined by the operator product expansion, up to a multiple $α$ of $\partial_\pm^2T_{\pm\pm}$ that labels point-splitting prescriptions. Its transformation law under the conformal map to locally inertial coordinates gives the variance in closed form. The relative fluctuation scales as $1/c$, with $c$ the central charge. For the Schwarzschild black hole it is $22/5c$ for the Hawking flux at infinity, that of flat-space thermal radiation. At the horizon it is $-44α/3c$ for the outgoing flux and $(238+720α)/5c$ for the ingoing flux that drives the backreaction. Both reduce to $22/5c$ for $α=-3/10$, which corresponds to the Möbius-covariant prescription. For large $c$ these results support the semiclassical approximation.

Nonabelianization map for the $W_{3,c=2}$ conformal blocks. Part I
Qianyu Hao 2611.31232v1 [PDF] Score 4
CFThep-th
This work is inspired by the nonabelianization construction of Virasoro conformal blocks. We start the process of defining the nonabelianization map which expresses $c=2$ $W_3$-algebra conformal blocks on a Riemann surface $C$ from Heisenberg blocks on a branched triple cover $\tilde{C}$ of $C$. This generalized nonabelianization map uses the datum of a $\mathfrak{gl}_3$ spectral network on $C$ and we find that this map also depends on the soliton degeneracies corresponding to the spectral network even though the soliton data didn't show up explicitly in the Virasoro case. This nonabelianization construction defines new formulas for $W_3$-algebra conformal blocks on $C$ in terms of fermion correlation functions determined by the Heisenberg blocks on $\tilde{C}$. We provide a check of the proposed construction associated to the more challenging $(123)$ type branch points which start to show up in three-fold covers. To be more precise, the simplest example that we focus on has only two of the $(123)$ type branch points and we show that in this example, our nonabelianization construction gives the desired two-point correlator and $τ$-function.

Constraining Regular Black Hole Models with the Effective Field Theory treatment of General Relativity
Sami Viollet 2611.30616v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravitygr-qc
In this paper, we present a method for using perturbative predictions from treating general relativity as an effective field theory to constrain non-perturbative approaches to quantum gravity in black hole contexts. We start by reviewing a known result from this framework, which estimates the first quantum corrections to the interaction energy of a static particle near a black hole. We then propose a way to test non-perturbative quantum black hole models - specifically those formulated as modified Schwarzschild metrics, also known as regular black holes - against this perturbative result. To illustrate, we apply our method to the Hayward metric, showing that it does not align with the perturbative prediction, and we identify the most general metric that can reproduce the expected result.
