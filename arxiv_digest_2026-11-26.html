Flat Space Entanglement from Wedge Holography
Robert C. Myers, Bruno Sivilotti 2611.31414v1 [PDF] Score 27
AdS/CFTCFTCelestial HolographyEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
We study holographic entanglement entropy in three-dimensional flat space by combining wedge holography with a controlled flat limit of AdS end-of-the-world branes. The limit takes a wedge of AdS$_4$ bounded by two AdS$_3$ branes to a Randall-Sundrum-like configuration of two flat branes, and the entanglement entropy of an interval in the two-dimensional corner CFT becomes that of an interval at null infinity of the branes. The result has the functional form of the entanglement entropy of a two-dimensional Carrollian CFT, the proposed holographic dual of gravity in asymptotically flat three-dimensional spacetimes. However, the inferred central charges yield $c_M=0$ and $c_L\to\infty$ in the flat limit. Adding a gravitational Chern-Simons term produces a $c_M$-like contribution, but we still find $c_L\to\infty$ and $c_M\to0$ in this limit. Moreover, the corresponding extremal surfaces do not approach the swing surfaces proposed by Song and collaborators. Instead, they collapse onto null rays along null infinity. We argue that both features are generic for flat limits of AdS/CFT. They follow from entanglement entropy being a property of the domain of dependence and from the equality of causal and entanglement wedges for intervals in the vacuum.

Soumen Pari 2611.32438v1 [PDF] Score 15
Entanglement & GeometryGeometric GravityQuantum Gravityhep-thgr-qc
We investigate the information paradox for one-sided asymptotically flat rotating Kerr black holes formed through gravitational collapse using the island prescription. Near the horizon, the dynamics of a scalar field can be described by an effective two-dimensional theory, which provides the framework for our analysis. We study how rotation affects the location of the island boundary $\partial I$, the Page time, and the scrambling time, treating non-extremal and extremal Kerr black holes separately. For non-extremal black holes, the emergence of an island prevents the unbounded growth of Hawking radiation entropy at late times and restores the unitary Page curve.
