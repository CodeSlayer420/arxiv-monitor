Are Black Hole and Cosmological Horizon Thermodynamics Necessarily Inequivalent?
Zachary J. Hoelscher 2610.39048v1 [PDF] Score 11
Geometric GravityQuantum GravityString Theoryhep-thastro-ph.COgr-qc
It has recently been argued that cosmological horizon thermodynamics and black hole thermodynamics are generically inequivalent in any consistent, UV complete theory of quantum gravity, where it was suggested that this could be falsified by the identification of a counterexample. This conjecture was motivated in part by noting that cosmological horizons do not incorporate many of the convenient features found in the black holes described by string theory. In this work, we consider as a toy model a universe with a single non-rotating black hole and a positive cosmological constant. We work in the Nariai limit where the mass of the black hole is taken to its maximum possible value, and where we also assert that the quantum field theory living on this background is strictly parity invariant. In the semi-classical regime where we neglect instability of the exact Nariai equilibrium under quantum fluctuations, we find that the observer cannot readily distinguish the physics in the neighborhood of the black hole horizon from the physics in the neighborhood of the cosmological horizon. This does not prove that the two are generically equivalent in any universe, though it does suggest that they cannot be entirely unrelated in general. We propose that any consistent theory of quantum gravity that aims to describe black holes in a universe with a positive cosmological constant should provide a related microscopic origin for the leading semiclassical cosmological entropy and black hole entropy in the neighborhood of the particular limit considered here.

AdS/CFTCelestial Holographyhep-thgr-qc
We reconstruct linearized gravitational perturbations in $AdS_{4}$ from a conserved, traceless stress tensor on a fixed conformally flat boundary metric, extending our earlier real Lorentzian construction. For admissible data with no spacelike Fourier support in a flat conformal frame, the physical response reduces to an integral over boundary light-cone cuts. Siqing Yu 2610.40371v1 [PDF] Score 6
AdS/CFTCFThep-thcond-mat.str-elgr-qc
The Abelian Higgs model in AdS$_5$, in the probe limit, is a standard holographic model of a strongly coupled superfluid, usually solved numerically. When the scalar mass saturates the Breitenlohner-Freedman bound and the quartic coupling takes the Bogomolny value, its condensed phase can be found in closed form on every static, uncharged AdS$_5$ background with maximally symmetric spatial sections. We prove that it is linearly stable, as is any nodeless probe-limit condensate in the standard quantization with a nonnegative quartic coupling and no superflow. On each slicing it answers a different question about the dual conformal field theory. Its excitations include towers that the superfluid effective theory does not describe; they come from an infrared fixed point of the probe sector. The hyperbolic slicing gives in closed form a homogeneous hairy phase of the black holes that compute the charged Rényi entropies of a ball. Its condensate does not vanish at the entangling surface. When sourced by operators inserted there, the phase sets in at Rényi index exactly one at zero chemical potential, as found for a neutral scalar.

Ernesto Frodden, Diego Hidalgo, Francisco Manzano 2610.40529v1 [PDF] Score 5
Geometric Gravityhep-th
We establish the first law of black hole thermodynamics for the recently discovered Kerr swirling (KS) and accelerated Kerr swirling (AKS) black hole solutions. We also revisit the first law for the Kerr-Taub-NUT (KTN) spacetime. Guided by Noether's original insight that global symmetries give rise to conservation laws, we employ and adapt the surface charge method for computing the associated charges (mass and angular momentum) in the presence of spacetime string-like defects (conical singularities) along the axes of both KS and KTN black holes. We obtain finite integrated charges in a quasi-local manner, without relying on the non-flat asymptotic regions characteristic of these spacetimes. We then establish a first law without introducing independent stringy terms unrelated to symmetries. In stark contrast, for the AKS spacetime, which can be regularized outside the singularity and lacks string-like defects, we find that the charges are consistent with a first law having the usual one-fourth of the area entropy. We first determine the mass using the Christodoulou-Ruffini formula and then check its agreement with the result obtained from the surface charge method.

Ian Le Meur, Fiona K. Seibold 2610.40386v1 [PDF] Score 5

We construct elliptic deformations of the $AdS_3 \times S^3 \times T^4$ superstring supported by mixed NSNS and RR fluxes in type IIB supergravity and investigate their integrability. We introduce virasoro_blocks, software that computes Virasoro conformal blocks at any central charge in a form suitable for the numerical bootstrap.
