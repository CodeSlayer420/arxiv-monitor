Flat Space Entanglement from Wedge Holography
Robert C. Myers, Bruno Sivilotti 2611.31414v1 [PDF] Score 27
AdS/CFTCFTCelestial HolographyEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
We study holographic entanglement entropy in three-dimensional flat space by combining wedge holography with a controlled flat limit of AdS end-of-the-world branes. The limit takes a wedge of AdS$_4$ bounded by two AdS$_3$ branes to a Randall-Sundrum-like configuration of two flat branes, and the entanglement entropy of an interval in the two-dimensional corner CFT becomes that of an interval at null infinity of the branes. The result has the functional form of the entanglement entropy of a two-dimensional Carrollian CFT, the proposed holographic dual of gravity in asymptotically flat three-dimensional spacetimes. However, the inferred central charges yield $c_M=0$ and $c_L\to\infty$ in the flat limit. Adding a gravitational Chern-Simons term produces a $c_M$-like contribution, but we still find $c_L\to\infty$ and $c_M\to0$ in this limit. Moreover, the corresponding extremal surfaces do not approach the swing surfaces proposed by Song and collaborators. Instead, they collapse onto null rays along null infinity. We argue that both features are generic for flat limits of AdS/CFT. They follow from entanglement entropy being a property of the domain of dependence and from the equality of causal and entanglement wedges for intervals in the vacuum.

We investigate the information paradox for one-sided asymptotically flat rotating Kerr black holes formed through gravitational collapse using the island prescription. Near the horizon, the dynamics of a scalar field can be described by an effective two-dimensional theory, which provides the framework for our analysis. We study how rotation affects the location of the island boundary $\partial I$, the Page time, and the scrambling time, treating non-extremal and extremal Kerr black holes separately. Propagation estimates then allow us to resolve successive breakups by induction on the block rank. The incoming and outgoing wave operators each have range equal to the absolutely continuous subspace, and the singular-continuous subspace vanishes. The scattering operator is thus unitary when each constituent can occupy any of its internal bound states. For a normalized incoming state of zero-energy constituents, the outgoing probability missing from the zero-energy channels equals the inclusive probability of producing at least one constituent with positive internal energy.

Constraining Regular Black Hole Models with the Effective Field Theory treatment of General Relativity
Sami Viollet 2611.30616v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravitygr-qc
In this paper, we present a method for using perturbative predictions from treating general relativity as an effective field theory to constrain non-perturbative approaches to quantum gravity in black hole contexts. We start by reviewing a known result from this framework, which estimates the first quantum corrections to the interaction energy of a static particle near a black hole. We then propose a way to test non-perturbative quantum black hole models - specifically those formulated as modified Schwarzschild metrics, also known as regular black holes - against this perturbative result. To illustrate, we apply our method to the Hayward metric, showing that it does not align with the perturbative prediction, and we identify the most general metric that can reproduce the expected result.
