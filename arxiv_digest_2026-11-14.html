Swing Surfaces in AdS/CFT
Jacqueline Caminiti, Sabrina Pasterski 2611.15854v1 [PDF] Score 29
AdS/CFTCFTEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
We study holographic entanglement entropy in the flat limit of AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$, and clarify the relation between Ryu-Takayanagi surfaces and the swing surfaces proposed for flat-space holography. We show that the ordinary RT formula does not admit a well-defined flat-space limit and connect this to subtleties of the state-operator map in the flat limit. We then construct an AdS$_3$ uplift of the swing prescription and develop its corresponding information-theoretic interpretation through two complementary approaches. 1-loop partition function of 11d supergravity on $\mathrm{AdS}_4\times S^7/\mathbb{Z}_k$: matching $A(k)$ in ABJM free energy

AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-th
The function $A(k)$ appearing in the large $N$ expansion of free energy of ABJM theory on $S^3$ is known exactly from localization. An earlier calculation of the 1-loop partition function of 11d supergravity on $\mathrm{AdS}_4\times S^7/\mathbb Z_k$, based on the standard KK spectrum on $S^7$, did not reproduce it. We identify a missing 1-loop supergravity contribution as that of the 3-form field fluctuations built from square-integrable harmonic 2-forms on Euclidean $\mathrm{AdS}_4$ tensored with $S^7$ modes, which are not captured by the KK spectrum. Adding it to the KK tower contribution expressed in terms of superconformal characters and applying the $\mathbb{Z}_k$ projection, the total expression simplifies, and its evaluation in a zeta-function prescription reproduces the expression for $A(k)$. We also apply a similar approach to the 3d ADHM theory whose M-theory dual is the orbifold $\mathrm{AdS}_4\times S^7/\mathbb{Z}_p$, which has singularity along the fixed locus $\mathrm{AdS}_4\times S^3$, supporting a 7d $SU(p)$ vector multiplet. Adding the localized KK modes of this multiplet to the $\mathbb{Z}_p$-projected supergravity spectrum gives a 1-loop free energy that reproduces the ADHM localization counterpart of $A(k)$.

We propose that the large $N$ transseries of a holographic observable above the black hole threshold admits a precision bound, a limit on how precisely the transseries can determine the observable at finite $N$, set by the data of its D-brane instanton sectors. We investigate inducing data for three dimensional asymptotically flat BMS gravity on hyperbolic Virasoro orbits with winding, which contain no constant supermomentum, and the restriction of formal smooth equivariant sections to global Lorentz orbits. Holographic Counterterms from Restricted Weyl Symmetry in Even-Dimensional Einstein-AdS Gravity
Giorgos Anastasiou 2611.16187v1 [PDF] Score 6
AdS/CFThep-thgr-qc
We develop a symmetry-based approach to renormalization for even-dimensional Einstein-AdS gravity based on infinitesimal bulk Weyl transformations that preserve its field equations asymptotically. We determine a bulk-covariant completion of the Einstein-Hilbert density by requiring its variation under restricted Weyl transformations with parameter $Σ(X)$ to vanish modulo terms whose integrals remain finite as the radial cutoff is removed. This requirement induces two types of compensating terms with fixed couplings: the Euler density and a hierarchy of covariant total derivatives. The resulting recursive relations admit a factorized solution in terms of shifted Laplacians, yielding a closed-form completion in arbitrary even bulk dimensions. On Einstein backgrounds, these expressions agree, after matching normalizations, with those obtained through the ambient construction of conformal invariants. The associated renormalized-volume identity establishes on-shell finiteness for Euclidean AlAdS Einstein geometries in arbitrary even dimensions. The prescription reproduces the known conformally renormalized actions in four, six and eight dimensions and provides an explicit ten-dimensional completion involving powers of the Laplacian up to $\Box^3$. Applications to eight- and ten-dimensional AdS black holes with non-conformally-flat Einstein horizons yield finite on-shell actions in agreement with Holographic Renormalization.

AdS/CFThep-thcond-mat.stat-mechmath-ph
In this work, we extend the equilibrium-evolution duality between stochastic quantization and Wilsonian renormalization to non-abelian gauge theories in anti-de Sitter space, for which the radial coordinate of AdS plays the role of the stochastic time. We show, to all orders in the fields for bulk theories without explicit radial dependence, that the Langevin distribution at time $t$ is the product of the ultraviolet and infrared radial wavefunctions at the cutoff $ε=t$. We therefore identify this Langevin process as the ground-state (Doob) transform of the radial evolution. Although our explicit results are provided at tree-level and derived in AdS$_4$, we also comment on the de Sitter counterpart of these statements and on the loop corrections.

We study Mpemba relaxation and its preparation cost in a holographic superconductor with full backreaction. We define the subsystem flow, which measures the departure from this error correction regime. Away from autonomy, the infinitesimal flow quantifies relative-entropy loss, while the history algebra and persistent core describe which observables the modular orbit generates and retains. We call an inclusion ergodic when modular orbits generate everything and retain nothing.
