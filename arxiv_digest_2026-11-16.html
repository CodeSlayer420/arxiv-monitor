Exploring M-theory on AdS$_7\times$S$^4$: Mellin amplitudes with composite operators
Francesco Aprile, Hector Puerta-Ramisa, Mitchell Woolley 2611.18472v1 [PDF] Score 13
AdS/CFTCFTString Theoryhep-th
We bootstrap mixed correlators in M-theory on AdS$_7\times$S$^4$, focusing on tree-level correlation functions involving one double-particle operator and three single-particle operators. Combining inputs from AdS/CFT, the chiral algebra, and the OPE at large central charge, we uniquely determine the Mellin amplitude for $\langle [{\cal O}_{2}{\cal O}_{q}]{\cal O}_{2}{\cal O}_{p}{\cal O}_{p+q-2}\rangle$ for generic $p$ and $q$. While our main focus is the $\mathcal{N}=(2,0)$ SCFT in six dimensions, we also derive the analogous result in AdS$_5\times$S$^5$, applying the same bootstrap approach to $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM. Despite their different dynamics, the resulting Mellin amplitudes exhibit a strikingly similar structure, with non-trivial OPE predictions for low-twist protected operators providing a key input. Our work is motivated by recent developments in the study of Kaluza-Klein correlators in bubbling geometries, where four-point functions involving single- and multi-particle operators are computed as response functions to smooth deformations of the vacuum geometry. While well tested in AdS$_5\times$S$^5$, we aim to extend this framework to M-theory.
#2
Infinite Index Identities for Argyres-Douglas Theories and Graded Vector Space Isomorphisms for VOAs
Hongliang Jiang 2611.18198v1 [PDF] Score 4
CFThep-thmath-phmath.QA
Supersymmetric indices provide stringent tests of dualities between superconformal field theories (SCFTs). We establish infinite families of Schur-index identities among four-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ Argyres-Douglas (AD) theories, with the logarithms of their fugacities related linearly. These identities connect theories with markedly different physical properties without requiring infrared duality, while still implying simple relations among their central charges and flavor levels. Through the SCFT/VOA correspondence, they become character identities for the associated affine and $W$-algebras. One broad family relates $D_p(G)$ and $(A_{(h+1)(p-1)},G)$ theories, corresponding to affine and principal $W$-algebras at boundary admissible levels. At the level of the underlying state spaces, these character identities suggest that the corresponding VOAs are related by graded vector space isomorphisms, which are weaker than full VOA isomorphisms. We explicitly construct such a graded isomorphism between the VOAs associated with $(A_1,A_{4p-3})$ and $(A_1,D_{2p})$. We also show the correspondence for universal affine and principal $W$-algebras and test its compatibility with their simple quotients by matching the lowest-degree null states. We establish analogous character identities for certain non-vacuum modules in concrete examples and conjecture a broader extension. Finally, beyond the SCFT/VOA correspondence, we discover similar identities between level-one affine and Virasoro algebras, whose further generalizations point toward a new spectral level-rank duality in WZW models.
#3
From pure spinor modules to superspace superfields
Ondrej Hulik, Raphael Senghaas 2611.18556v1 [PDF] Score 3
Asymptotic Symmetrieshep-thmath-ph
We relate the pure spinor formalism to the superspace description of constrained superfields and gauge multiplets, mainly for abelian two-dimensional N=(2,2) supersymmetry, with three- and four-dimensional N=1 examples for comparison. For each multiplet we give an explicit module over the supertranslation Chevalley-Eilenberg algebra and match its reconstruction with the known superfields. First-order constraints come from modules supported on linear subspaces of the nilpotence variety, and higher-order ones, such as the complex-linear constraint, from Yoneda products of their Ext classes. Requiring a connection to act on given matter and its conjugate restricts its curvature, which yields the ordinary, twisted and semichiral vector multiplets with their field strengths. The module of each vector multiplet is the fiber product over the origin of the coordinate rings of the matter supports: its potentials are differences of partial potentials, and its gauge parameters are the matter superfields. For the large vector multiplet this fiber product is not a quotient of the pure spinor ring; its field strengths are four odd semichiral superfields, and partial regluing gives its constrained versions.

Geometric Quantization of the Giant Graviton Master Moduli Space
Kasia Budzik, Davide Gaiotto 2611.18539v1 [PDF] Score 3
AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-thmath-ph
We describe a remarkable reorganization of the space of gauge invariant operators of free or twisted $U(N)$ gauge theories as combinations of single-trace and determinant operators modulo certain "open loop equations". The reorganization has a transparent holographic interpretation, describing how states at finite $N$ can be reproduced by imposing certain $N$-independent gauge constraints on the combination of the closed strings Fock space and a geometric quantization of the moduli space of Giant Graviton D-branes, with quantization parameter $N^{-1}$. The moduli space of Giant Graviton D-branes is reproduced from tachyon condensation of auxiliary D-brane/anti-D-brane pairs which are "Koszul dual" to the D-brane system which supports the original gauge theory. We describe how to impose the open loop equations via certain BRST complexes and conjecture that their homology recovers the finite $N$ BPS Hilbert space in the limit of large auxiliary rank. The BRST construction conjecturally categorifies Murthy's Giant Graviton Expansion of indices counting $U(N)$-invariant BPS operators.

Entropic quantum error correction for independent and correlated amplitude damping errors
Gang Zhou 2611.18192v1 [PDF] Score 3
Entanglement & Geometryquant-phmath-phmath.OA
The idea of using quantum entropy for quantum error correction (QEC) has existed for a long time. However, its computational complexity has limited its practical use: even a $[[4,1]]$ code for standard independent amplitude damping errors involves a $256\times 256$ Hermitian matrix, so the approach scales poorly as the codeword length increases. For correlated errors, the situation is even worse. In this paper, we show that, by using the insights developed in our previous paper and exploiting certain structures of quantum conditional mutual information, we can solve these problems exactly by hand, using only $2\times 2$ unitary matrices. By examining the entropy, we identify the leakage channels and the amount of leakage exactly, then define the recovery map accordingly. The rules defining our recovery map are simple, intuitive, and computationally inexpensive: for independent errors, it is based on 16 explicitly given orthonormal vectors and 6 instructions; for correlated errors, it is based on 6 explicitly given orthonormal vectors and 6 instructions. Our method sacrifices little in terms of performance: for the widely used entanglement fidelity, our simple QEC recovery map achieves a sharp result; for the worst-case fidelity, the best result is about $9\%$ better than ours; for correlated errors, there are no comparable results for short codewords. Among the known techniques, our recovery map is the first non-global one. The calculations use elementary linear algebra and calculus, and is presented in great detail.
