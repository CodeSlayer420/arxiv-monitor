#1
A precision bound for gravitational transseries
Ji Hoon Lee 2611.17274v1 [PDF] Score 6
AdS/CFTEntanglement & GeometryString Theoryhep-th
We propose that the large $N$ transseries of a holographic observable above the black hole threshold admits a precision bound, a limit on how precisely the transseries can determine the observable at finite $N$, set by the data of its D-brane instanton sectors. In doing so, we develop methods in large $N$ resurgence, based on the work of Écalle, that capture the $e^{-N^2}$ asymptotics of gravitational saddles beyond the $e^{-N}$ instantons. Under an assumption on the growth of the D-brane instanton sums that we expect in the black hole phase, we find an analytic obstruction to the resummation of the large $N$ transseries. This obstruction sets the precision bound. As our main example and consistency check, we study the $\frac{1}{16}$-BPS index of $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang-Mills and its black hole. We derive relations between the D-brane instanton data on thermal AdS and on the black hole, in the form of a duality that exchanges the numbers of D-brane instantons on the two backgrounds. In the index, these relations reproduce the actions of D3-branes wrapped on the black hole. We conclude with our expectations for generic situations and their relation to the double-cone wormhole.
#2
Twisting IKKT with branes
Fabian Hahner, Natalie M. Paquette 2611.17270v1 [PDF] Score 6
String Theoryhep-thmath-ph
Motivated by the conjectural relationship between the IKKT matrix model and type IIB string theory, we classify supersymmetric twists of the matrix model, allowing for both dynamical and kinematical supercharges. Beyond the two twists available at any finite $N$, which we studied in our previous work, we find five additional classes at infinite rank which require non-commuting backgrounds associated with D-branes in IIB supergravity. Together, these twists realize representatives of every equivalence class of twisting supercharges in flat-space type IIB supergravity recently classified by the first author; however, the precise form of the orbit stratification differs. This difference is related to a higher central extension of the IIB superalgebra that appears in our treatment of the IKKT model in the BV formalism. Expanding around the non-commutative backgrounds gives worldvolume theories on products of A- and B-branes, including four-dimensional BF theory on an emergent D3-brane. We relate all these twists to deformations of BCOV theory (the closed string field theory of the topological B-model) using the open-closed duality of classical string field theory. For one of the twists with Spin$(7)$ symmetry, we also show the localization of the IKKT partition function by Moore, Nekrasov, and Shatashvili can be directly reconstructed with the BV formalism, with their auxiliary field completion encoded by quadratic antifield terms. Throughout, we provide self-contained treatments of the formal techniques we employ.
#3
Finite cyclic monodromy in BMS3 gravity
Evangelos Melas 2611.16823v1 [PDF] Score 6
CFTGeometric GravityQuantum Gravityhep-thmath-ph
We investigate inducing data for three dimensional asymptotically flat BMS gravity on hyperbolic Virasoro orbits with winding, which contain no constant supermomentum, and the restriction of formal smooth equivariant sections to global Lorentz orbits. Using the developing map of the associated Hill operator we rederive their stabilizer and show that, in the spin double cover, it is the direct product of a line with a cyclic group of even order. The character of the cyclic factor is therefore a discrete inducing label independent of the continuous one, unlike for the massive BMS particles, where it is fixed by the spin. At points with the corresponding finite rotational symmetry, restriction of formal smooth equivariant sections to a global Lorentz orbit yields the finite cyclic equivariance condition, while an independently defined unitary global branch realizes the associated finite difference equation. At points with timelike projected momentum we derive the resulting ladder of Poincare helicities, show that the projected rest energy is not an orbit invariant, construct locally flat winding BMS geometries with hyperbolic Lorentz holonomy, prove that the smooth real periodic near horizon current model excludes these winding orbits, and obtain selection rules and closed form expressions for finite monodromy resolved orbital functionals. A unitary realization on the full infinite dimensional orbit is not constructed, and the interpretation of the cyclic sectors in quantum gravity remains conjectural.
#4
De Sitter Gravity and Emergent Time from Fusion Matrix Product States and SYK
Shinsei Ryu, Yu-ki Suzuki 2611.17045v1 [PDF] Score 5
CFTQuantum Gravityhep-thcond-mat.str-elgr-qc
We propose a quantum-mechanical model dual to two-dimensional de Sitter (dS) gravity. It is a quantum channel, defined by a matrix product state that fuses one spin at each step, and is related to a limit of the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev model without chord crossings. Its simplest part is a random walk with an emergent $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ symmetry, which we identify with the symmetry of the future boundary of dS. It selects a time variable in which the walk obeys the Schwarzian action and yields the disk amplitude of dS Jackiw-Teitelboim gravity. As the simplest case, we consider free massless scalar fields in the fixed background of dS$_2$. We find the agreement of the two-point function in the Bunch-Davies vacuum and the matching of symmetry. Our construction provides a concrete model for the dS/CFT correspondence for two-dimensional de Sitter gravity.
#5
Bound States of Two D0-Branes and the Three-Graviton Coupling
Sergio Sanjurjo, Themistocles Zikopoulos 2611.17325v1 [PDF] Score 4
AdS/CFTString Theoryhep-th
We numerically solve for the ground state of the $SU(2)$ BFSS matrix model for M-theory. This state corresponds to the zero-energy bound state of two D0-branes or, by the BFSS conjecture, to the eleven-dimensional graviton with two units of momentum along the M-theory circle. The wavefunction exhibits a strongly coupled core, as well as an asymptotic power-law tail where the D0-branes are well separated. The coefficient of this power-law decay is the three-graviton coupling, previously computed by Herderschee and Maldacena, which our numerical solution reproduces with high accuracy. We also show that this coupling is protected by supersymmetry in the matrix model, and derive its value from the non-renormalized one-loop interaction of the two D0-branes. Lastly, we provide a simple representation-theoretic argument regarding the recently proposed excited bound states of the model.
