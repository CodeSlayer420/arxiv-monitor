Modular Flow and Holographic Timelike Entanglement Entropy in AdS and Flat Holography
Mehdi Hakami Shalamzari, Zahra Paygozar, Mahdi Kord Zangeneh 2610.17165v1 [PDF] Score 25
AdS/CFTCFTCelestial HolographyEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
Timelike entanglement entropy provides a useful probe of holographic theoris, yet an explicit bulk realization of the modular flow underlying its timelike extremal curve construction has so far been lacking. We construct modular flows associated with spacelike and timelike intervals in AdS$_{3}$/CFT$_{2}$ and extend this framework to flat$_{3}$/CCFT$_{2}$. Beginning with the boundary symmetry generators, we explicitly derive the boundary modular flows and extend them into the bulk, providing a systematic procedure that relates bulk modular flows to the extremal curves and holographic (timelike) entanglement entropy. For spacelike intervals, the resulting bulk modular flows determine the extremal curves, whose integrated line elements reproduce the standard holographic entanglement entropy. We then generalize this approach to timelike intervals, explicitly constructing the modular flows whose fixed points reproduce the extremal spacelike and timelike curves which gives the holographic timelike entanglement entropy. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first explicit construction of the modular flow associated with a timelike extremal curve for a timelike interval. The same procedure is then carried over to flat$_{3}$/CCFT$_{2}$, which give rise to the holographic (timelike) entanglement entropy. Finally, we show that the flat space results obtained in this work can be recovered from their AdS$_3$ counterparts in the flat space limit.

Holographic Pseudo Entropy from the Rindler Method in AdS and Flat Space
Mehdi Hakami Shalamzari, Zahra Paygozar, Mahdi Kord Zangeneh 2610.17239v1 [PDF] Score 18
AdS/CFTCFTCelestial HolographyEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
Via the Rindler method, we study holographic timelike entanglement entropy (TEE) for a general timelike interval in AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$ and flat$_3$/CCFT$_2$. The TEE is the pseudo entropy of a timelike interval. In the bulk, the imaginary part of the TEE comes from a timelike geodesic, but in the Rindler picture this geodesic has not been identified. After a Rindler transformation to a rotating BTZ black hole, the two spacelike extremal curves lie on the inner and outer horizons, and exchanging $l_u\leftrightarrow \ell l_φ$ takes one to the other. We find the geometric meaning of the timelike extremal curve: a geodesic stretched between the two horizons. Its length, $iπ\ell$, is independent of the interval, and it alone produces the imaginary part $iπc/6$. We follow the same procedure in flat space holography, where the spacelike curve sits on the cosmological horizon and the timelike curve lies between the cosmological horizon and timelike infinity. Evaluating the holographic TEE for a timelike interval in CCFT$_2$ on the plane, we find that, in contrast with the cylinder, the real and imaginary parts are equal.

Realisation of de Sitter space as an excited state in string theory
Archana Maji 2610.20301v1 [PDF] Score 9
Quantum GravityString Theoryhep-th
String theory is widely regarded as the only plausible candidate for a consistent UV-complete theory of quantum gravity. Cosmological observations suggest that our universe is undergoing accelerated expansion, pointing towards the presence of a small positive cosmological constant. Since de Sitter space is the maximally symmetric solution of Einstein's equations with a positive cosmological constant, understanding whether four-dimensional de Sitter space can be realized within string theory has become one of the central questions. Following a series of works that realize de Sitter space as an excited state---namely, as a Glauber--Sudarshan (GS) state in type IIB string theory---our goal has been to extend such a construction to heterotic string theories. Realizing de Sitter space as an excited state over a time-independent supersymmetric Minkowski background provides a mechanism for evading the vacuum-based no-go theorems that obstruct de Sitter solutions in string theory. Starting from a generic M-theory configuration, we obtain four-dimensional de Sitter space in the late-time limit of flat-slicing coordinates in both heterotic $SO(32)$ and heterotic $E_8 \times E_8$ theories through appropriate dynamical duality sequences. The corresponding GS state is constructed by evaluating the expectation value of the metric operator in M-theory using path-integral techniques. We analyse the conditions required for the existence of a well-defined effective field theory description of the setup and find that these conditions are equivalent to satisfying the null energy condition in a 3+1-dimensional FLRW cosmology. Furthermore, the de Sitter GS states are found to lie well within the temporal bound imposed by the Trans-Planckian Censorship Conjecture, rendering them as transient states. In the case of $E_8\times E_8$ theory, we also investigate implications arising from axion cosmology.

Thermal AdS two-point functions and loop induced effective one-point vertices
Astha Kakkar 2610.20535v1 [PDF] Score 8
AdS/CFTCFThep-th
We present a study of thermal two-point functions from Witten diagrams in thermal AdS$_{d+1}$, defined with the conformal boundary $S_β^1 \times \mathbb{R}^{d-1}$, with an aim to establish a connection between thermal CFT data and perturbative bulk dynamics. We analyze one-loop tadpole and bench diagrams arising from the cubic $φφχ$ interaction in the bulk, segregating the different relative thermal winding sectors for the bench, and also extend the analysis to include quartic scalar contact diagrams with and without derivative interactions. We also analyze the spinning exchange thermal two-point function. Utilizing the split representation of bulk propagators and the method of images we reorganize the thermal Witten diagrams into the standard AdS integrals and obtain spectral and image-sum representations that make KMS periodicity of the correlators manifest. Additionally, we show possible position dependent effective one-point vertices of scalar and spinning fields in the bulk arising from the reduction of the coincident- point thermal bulk to bulk propagator loop, allowing tadpole-type diagrams to be reconstructed from effective local bulk interactions. The bench diagram however retains an intrinsically bi-local structure. We further investigate the thermal one-point coefficients and perturbative OPE data encoded in these thermal correlators, including contributions associated with anomalous dimensions. Finally, we examine their relation to thermal dispersion relations and the role of contributions that are not determined by discontinuity data alone. Our results provide a set of explicit bulk constructions for exploring the analytic structure of thermal CFT correlators and their connection to the thermal conformal bootstrap.

We take a first step in identifying the superconformal quantum mechanics dual to supersymmetric AdS$_2\times$S$^2$ solutions. We focus on the Type IIB solutions of the form AdS$_2\times$S$^2\times$CY$_2\timesΣ$ that preserve eight supercharges, with $Σ$ a two-dimensional space parametrised by $(x_1,x_2)$, and read off their brane content, a D1-D5-NS5 intersection with D3 and D7 flavour branes. We develop the post-Newtonian framework for the near-zone dynamics of a binary in de Sitter and anti-de Sitter spacetime. We consider a binary interacting via a free scalar field in a fixed (A)dS background in $D+1$ spacetime dimensions, with $D\ge3$ and odd. Using multipole expansion and re-summation techniques, we solve for the field degrees of freedom to obtain an effective particle description in the form of a Fokker action. Pseudo-spectral methods in gravitational lensing: Spin precession in loop quantum black hole spacetimes
Xiankai Pang, Xiao-Kan Guo 2610.18064v2 [PDF] Score 7
Quantum Gravitygr-qc
Gravitational lensing is usually treated as an initial value problem: for a given impact parameter of an unbound orbit, one computes the resulting deflection angle. In this paper we advocate the inverse point of view. For a prescribed deflection angle, the orbit is selected by two boundary conditions at spatial infinity, so that the lensing problem becomes a well-defined two-point boundary value problem, with the impact parameter playing the role of a nonlinear eigenvalue. We show that pseudo-spectral methods based on Chebyshev collocation solve this boundary value problem accurately at modest computational cost, and that the parallel transport equations governing the spin precession of the particle reduce to a single linear system on the same grid. As a concrete application, we compute the spin precession of massive particles in backward scattering off two black hole spacetimes from loop quantum gravity: the covariant effective metric and the self-dual loop quantum black hole. All equations are written for a general static, spherically symmetric line element with three free functions, so that both spacetimes are handled on the same footing. We find that the quantum corrections are most pronounced for slowly moving particles, while in the ultra-relativistic limit the universal spin flip is always recovered. The flip itself is universal, but the rate at which it is approached is not: the derivative of $\cosξ$ with respect to the particle speed at $v=1$ is a strong-field quantity in every backward channel and depends clearly on the quantum parameters of both spacetimes. The framework is not restricted to the models studied here and applies to any static, spherically symmetric spacetime.

M. Ali-Akbari, M. M. Daryaei Goki 2610.18716v1 [PDF] Score 5
AdS/CFTEntanglement & Geometryhep-th
We study the imaginary part of the holographic timelike entanglement entropy (HTEE) in a charged asymptotically AdS$_5$ black-hole background whose dual field theory exhibits a critical point in the presence of a chemical potential $μ$ and temperature $T$. Identifying the imaginary part of the HTEE with the imaginary part of the pseudoentropy, we derive an analytical expression for this quantity and investigate its behavior on the stable and unstable thermodynamic branches. We find that the imaginary HTEE vanishes in the uncharged limit and is induced entirely by a nonzero chemical potential, signaling the emergence of quantum coherence between the initial and final states. Gravity from entropy (GfE) identifies its Lagrangian with the geometric quantum relative entropy (GQRE) between the spacetime metric and a metric induced by matter and curvature, treating the metric itself as a quantum operator. We extend this construction to the Tsallis, Rényi, and Sharma-Mittal families, which we call gravity from generalized entropy (GfGE), and determine their common Einstein limit, weak-field spectra, auxiliary-field formulation, and horizon entropy. We also extend the volume-constraint cosmological constant to the full family; its leading entropy dependence is set by the scalar mass. A finite set of entropy-function derivatives controls the leading weak-curvature departures from general relativity.
