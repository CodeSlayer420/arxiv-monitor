Aspects of Codimension two Holography in de Sitter Space
Kanhu Kishore Nanda, Somnath Porey, Masataka Watanabe et al. 2610.22101v1 [PDF] Score 14
AdS/CFTCFTEntanglement & Geometryhep-thgr-qc
Following \cite{Yadav:2024ray} we embark on a further study of the codimension two holography in de Sitter (dS) space. The $d$ dimensional bulk dS space is sliced by two end of the world $(d-1)$ dimensional branes which are bestowed with either Lorentzian dS or Euclidean AdS metric joining at a common $(d-2)$ dimensional space where the dual CFT lives. Through explicit computations of the holographic entanglement entropy, partition function, and Renyi entropy in both slicings for the bulk dS space emphasizing their similarities as well as differences we provide further evidence for this duality. We also make comments on further generalization of the codimension two holography.

Segal's semigroup, Virasoro structure, and Ward identities in Compactified Imaginary Liouville Theory
Yang Xiao, Yuxiao Xie 2610.22170v1 [PDF] Score 13
CFTmath.PRmath-ph
Compactified Imaginary Liouville Theory (CILT for short) is a probabilistically constructed Conformal Field Theory (CFT), expected to have connections to minimal models, Coulomb gas integrals, and loop models. In this paper, we study the quantization of Segal's semigroup given by CILT. Each element $\mathbf T_f$ in the quantized semigroup corresponds to a conformal annulus: the unit disk $\mathbb D$ with $f(\mathbb D^\circ)$ removed, where $f:\mathbb D\to f(\mathbb D)\subset \mathbb D^\circ$ is a contracting biholomorphism. This semigroup encodes the local conformal structure of the CFT, in particular the Virasoro symmetry with central charge $<1$ for CILT. We first relate the quantized Segal's semigroup to Segal's amplitudes of annuli and recover the Virasoro generators $\mathbf L_n,\widetilde{\mathbf L}_n$ ($n\geq0$) of CILT as generators of this semigroup. Compared to the real Liouville CFT, the main difficulty here is the absence of positivity, which we address through estimates for the imaginary Gaussian Multiplicative Chaos and non-self-adjoint operators. Using Segal's gluing axiom as in the real case, we differentiate the amplitude and use conformal covariance to derive the Ward identities in terms of all Virasoro operators $\mathbf L_n,\widetilde{\mathbf L}_n$ ($n\in\mathbb Z$). We also outline the parallel theory for Boundary CILT (BCILT), concerning Segal's semigroup of semiannuli. These results provide essential tools for the conformal bootstrap program of (B)CILT, boundary BPZ equations, boundary structure constants, and the global construction of conformal blocks.

Bound States Inside the Horizon and Black Hole Entropy
Hassan Firouzjahi 2610.21426v1 [PDF] Score 7
Geometric GravityQuantum Gravitygr-qchep-th
We study the bound states perturbations in the interior of the Schwarzschild black hole and examine whether or not their microstates spectrum can furnish a statistical description of the black hole entropy. These states contain a discrete set of modes characterized by the labels $(s, p,\ell,m)$, where $s$ represents the spin of the species, $p$ denotes the radial excitation number while $(\ell,m)$ are the usual angular momentum labels. Requiring that the shortest wavelength resolved by the highly excited bound states remains above the quantum gravity scale introduces an angular cutoff $\ell_{\rm max}\sim r_H/L_{\rm Pl}$. The resulting angular degeneracy therefore scales with the horizon area. We count the corresponding configurations in the microcanonical ensemble and find an entropy of the form $S_{\rm bs}=αA/\hbar G$. Demanding that the bound state sector to be in thermal equilibrium with the black hole at the Hawking temperature fixes the fraction of the bound state energy in total black hole energy. For the gravitational perturbations sector, the equilibrium bound state contribution in entropy and the energy fraction are typically small. We also examine the conditions under which the bound states could account for the full black hole entropy. Although such saturation is possible at the level of state counting, it requires an order unity fraction in the black hole energy and therefore the backreactions on the background geometry cannot be ignored.

Operator-Space Entanglement Entropy in Random Fermionic Gaussian Dynamics
Pablo Bayona-Pena, Elena Gramellini, Lorenzo Piroli 2610.21723v1 [PDF] Score 7
Entanglement & GeometryQuantum Gravityquant-phcond-mat.stat-mechmath-ph
We study the von Neumann operator-space entanglement entropy (OSEE) of initial product operators evolved under random gaussian dynamics, as modeled by a Brownian SYK$_2$ model. By exploiting a mapping to a domain-wall melting problem, we derive an exact expression for the time-dependent bipartite OSEE in the thermodynamic limit, and obtain an analytic characterization of the corresponding gaussian Page curve emerging at late times. Our results build on recent work on the entanglement dynamics in the all-to-all quantum simple exclusion process, leveraging tools from RMT, including the Coulomb-gas approach and a mapping to the free Jacobi process. Finally, based on our results, we discuss the possibility of relating the OSEE with non-gaussianity measures in the Heisenberg picture.

Renormalization of Quantum Gravity with Lagrange Multiplier Fields
F. T. Brandt, J. Frenkel, S. Martins-Filho et al. 2610.21706v1 [PDF] Score 5
Quantum Gravityhep-thgr-qc
We study the renormalization of Einstein gravity coupled to a scalar field in the Lagrange multiplier (LM) formulation, where a pair of quantum LM fields, $(λ'_{μν},σ')$, is coupled to the gauge-fixed gravitational and scalar equations of motion. Using the fact that in this approach there are no higher loop corrections, we show that renormalizing the two quantum LM fields at one-loop order absorbs, off-shell, five out of the six independent divergent structures. The remaining off-shell counterterm, proportional to $ (\partial_μ\barφ\,\partial^μ\barφ)^2, $ is nonzero on shell and cannot be absorbed by any LM field redefinition, since such redefinitions generate only counterterms that vanish on shell. In contrast, in pure gravity (no matter), the LM renormalization absorbs all one-loop divergences and no additional counterterms are required. The LM formulation therefore provides a sharp off-shell separation between equation-of-motion divergences, absorbed into LM field renormalizations, and the single irreducible structure.

On Algebraic Background-Independence
Luka Vinck, Sebastian De Haro 2610.22042v1 [PDF] Score 3
Quantum Gravityphysics.hist-phgr-qchep-th
It has recently been claimed that algebraic methods can be used to give a background-independent description of semiclassical gravity. This paper aims to provide an entry point into recent algebraic approaches to quantum gravity and to analyse and critically assess the conception of background-independence here involved. We discuss an algebraic framework for formulating an algebra of observables along the worldline of an observer in a cosmological setting. By 'gravitationally dressing' the observables to the observer's worldline, the reference to the spacetime background can be set aside at the level of the abstract *-algebra. Thus this *-algebra is taken to be independent of any particular spacetime realisation, with different states on the algebra corresponding to different backgrounds. We compare this algebraic framework to three influential notions of background-independence in the literature and argue that none of these fully captures the sense in which the *-algebra is taken to be background-independent. Building on this, we extract a novel notion of algebraic background-independence tied to the representation-independence of the *-algebra. This algebraic conception motivates two further concepts of background-independence tailored to semiclassical theories: semiclassical background-independence and perturbative background-independence. We suggest that these provide useful tools for evaluating the scope and limitations of semiclassical approaches to quantum gravity.
